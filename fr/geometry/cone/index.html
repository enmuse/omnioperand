Governing Mathematical Formula:

Démonstration Mathématique Étape par Étape

1
Calculus Solid of Revolution Integral

Revolving the line y = (r/h)x from x = 0 to h around the x-axis: V = π ∫₀ʰ (r/h)² x² dx = π(r²/h²) · [h³ / 3] = ⅓ · π · r² · h. Exactly one-third of the circumscribed cylinder volume.

2
Pythagorean Slant Height

The apex, center, and perimeter form a right triangle: s = √(r² + h²).

3
Sector Unrolling for Lateral Area

The unrolled lateral surface forms a circular sector with radius s and arc length 2πr: A_lateral = π · r · s. Total Area = πrs + πr² = πr(s + r).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the volume of a cone exactly one-third of a cylinder? +
As proved rigorously by Eudoxus and Archimedes via exhaustion (and modern triple integration), any cone or pyramid with base area B and height h has volume V = ⅓ · B · h, regardless of base shape.
What is the angle of repose in conical granular piles? +
When dry aggregate (sand, gravel, grain) pours from a conveyor into a conical pile, internal friction limits the slope angle θ = arctan(h/r), known as the material's natural angle of repose (~34° for gravel).
