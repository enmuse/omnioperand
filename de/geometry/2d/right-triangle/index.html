Mathematical Rigor & Closed-Form Formulations

1 Pythagorean Theorem & Inversions

▼

For any triangle with a 90° interior angle:

a² + b² = c² ⟹ c = √(a² + b²)

Inversions for unknown legs:

a = √(c² - b²) , b = √(c² - a²)

2 Altitude to Hypotenuse & Geometric Mean Theorem

▼

Equating two formulations for triangle area:

Area = ½ · a · b = ½ · c · hc ⟹ hc = (a · b) / c

The altitude splits the hypotenuse into segments p = a² / c and q = b² / c, confirming Euclid's Geometric Mean Theorem:

hc² = p · q

3 Inradius & Circumradius Formulas

▼

By Thales's Theorem, the circumcenter of any right triangle lies exactly at the midpoint of the hypotenuse:

R = c / 2

The inradius ri is given by the ratio of area to semi-perimeter s = (a + b + c) / 2:

ri = Area / s = (a · b) / (a + b + c) = (a + b - c) / 2

Häufig Gestellte Fragen

What is the Pythagorean Theorem? +
The Pythagorean Theorem states that in any right Euclidean triangle with perpendicular legs a and b and hypotenuse c:
a² + b² = c² ⟹ c = √(a² + b²)
Subtracting yields the leg inversions: a = √(c² - b²) and b = √(c² - a²).
What is the formula for the altitude to the hypotenuse? +
Equating two expressions for triangle area (Area = ½ a · b = ½ c · hc) yields the exact altitude:
hc = (a · b) / c
Furthermore, by the Geometric Mean Theorem, hc² = p · q, where p and q are the segment projections onto the hypotenuse.
What is a special 30-60-90 right triangle? +
A 30-60-90 triangle is formed by bisecting an equilateral triangle. Its side lengths are always in the exact radical ratio 1 : √3 : 2. If the short leg is x, the long leg is x√3, and the hypotenuse is 2x.
What is an isosceles right triangle (45-45-90)? +
A 45-45-90 triangle is formed by cutting a square along its diagonal. Both legs are equal (a = b), and the hypotenuse is always c = a√2.
