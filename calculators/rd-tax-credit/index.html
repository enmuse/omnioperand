Frequently Asked Questions: Federal R&D Tax Credits

What expenses qualify for the federal R&D tax credit? +
Under IRC Section 41, Qualified Research Expenses (QRE) include: 100% of W-2 wages paid to employees performing or directly supervising technological research, 65% of fees paid to third-party US contractors performing research, and 100% of cloud server hosting (AWS/GCP/Azure) and supplies used in the research process.
How does the Qualified Small Business (QSB) payroll tax offset work? +
Under IRC Section 41(h), early-stage companies with less than $5 million in gross receipts and no gross receipts prior to the 5-taxable-year period can elect to apply up to $500,000 of their federal R&D credit directly against their employer payroll taxes (FICA/OASDI and Medicare), providing immediate cash liquidity even if the startup is unprofitable.
How is the Alternative Simplified Credit (ASC) calculated? +
The ASC equals 14% of current-year QRE that exceeds a base amount (50% of the average QRE for the prior 3 tax years). If the company has no prior 3-year research history, the credit equals a flat 6% of current-year QRE.
