Frequently Asked Questions: Commercial Underwriting

What is Debt Yield in commercial real estate lending? +
Debt Yield is the ratio of Net Operating Income (NOI) to the total commercial loan amount: Debt Yield = (NOI / Loan Amount) · 100%. Institutional CMBS and life insurance lenders prefer a minimum debt yield of 9.0% to 10.0%, as it measures the lender's cash-on-cash return if they were to foreclose on the property.
How is the Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate) calculated? +
Cap Rate = (Net Operating Income / Property Valuation or Purchase Price) · 100%. It represents the unleveraged rate of return expected on a commercial real estate asset based on its current operational cash flow.
What is the difference between GPR and EGI? +
Gross Potential Rent (GPR) is theoretical 100% occupancy rental revenue. Effective Gross Income (EGI) is the actual cash collected after subtracting market vacancy and credit collection losses, and adding auxiliary revenues such as parking, laundry, and utility reimbursements.
