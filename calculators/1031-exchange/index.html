Frequently Asked Questions: IRS Section 1031 Exchanges

What is 'boot' in a 1031 exchange? +
Boot is non-like-kind property received in an exchange that triggers immediate tax liability. Cash boot occurs when net sales cash is pocketed instead of reinvested. Mortgage boot occurs when the replacement property debt is lower than the debt paid off on the relinquished property, resulting in taxable debt relief.
What are the strict deadlines for a 1031 exchange? +
The investor has exactly 45 calendar days from the sale closing date to formally identify potential replacement properties in writing to the Qualified Intermediary (QI), and exactly 180 calendar days (or until the tax return due date, whichever is earlier) to complete the acquisition.
How is depreciation recapture taxed during a real estate sale? +
Under IRC Section 1250, all cumulative straight-line depreciation claimed on real property is unrecaptured and taxed at a maximum federal rate of 25%, plus state income taxes. A 1031 exchange completely defers this 25% recapture tax.
