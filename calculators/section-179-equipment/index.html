Frequently Asked Questions: Section 179 Equipment Expensing

Can you claim the Section 179 tax deduction if you finance or lease the equipment? +
Yes! This is one of the most powerful cash-flow strategies in the US tax code. Under IRC Section 179, you can deduct 100% of the qualifying equipment purchase price in the tax year it is placed in service, even if you paid only a small down payment and financed the remaining balance with an equipment loan or capital lease.
What are the Section 179 deduction and spending caps? +
For tax year 2024–2026, the maximum Section 179 deduction limit is $1,220,000. The spending cap phase-out threshold begins at $3,050,000 (dollar-for-dollar reduction). Any remaining eligible cost basis beyond Section 179 is eligible for bonus depreciation.
