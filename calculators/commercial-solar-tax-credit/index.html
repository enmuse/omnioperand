Frequently Asked Questions: Commercial Solar Tax Credits

How does the Section 48 Investment Tax Credit (ITC) work for commercial solar? +
Under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA), commercial businesses claiming the Section 48 ITC receive a dollar-for-dollar tax credit equal to 30% of the turnkey system cost (plus potential 10% bonus adders for domestic content or energy communities).
How is MACRS depreciation calculated on solar equipment? +
IRS rules require the depreciable basis of commercial solar assets to be reduced by 50% of the federal ITC claimed. If a 30% ITC is claimed, the depreciable basis is 100% - 15% = 85% of total system cost. This 85% basis is then depreciated over a 5-year MACRS recovery schedule (20% Year 1, 32% Year 2, 19.2% Year 3, 11.52% Year 4, 11.52% Year 5, 5.76% Year 6).
What is the typical commercial solar payback period? +
When combining the 30% ITC with Year 1 MACRS accelerated depreciation tax savings, businesses frequently recover 50% to 60% of total system costs in Year 1 tax relief alone, leading to a simple capital payback of 3.5 to 6.0 years.
