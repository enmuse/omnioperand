Governing Mathematical Formula:
DSCR = Gross Monthly Rent / Total Monthly PITIA
PITIA = P · [ r(1 + r)ⁿ ] / [ (1 + r)ⁿ - 1 ] + Taxes / 12 + Insurance / 12 + HOA

Step-by-Step Underwriting Derivation

1
Financed Principal Calculation

Loan Amount P = Purchase Price · (1 - Down%). For a $400,000 property with 20% down, P = $320,000.

2
Monthly Principal & Interest (P&I) Amortization

Monthly rate r = 0.0725 / 12 = 0.00604167 across n = 360 months. Compounding factor (1+r)³⁶⁰ = 8.7056. Monthly P&I = $320,000 · [r · 8.7056 / (8.7056 - 1)] = $2,182.88.

3
Monthly Escrows & Total PITIA

Escrowing annual taxes ($400/mo), insurance ($150/mo), and HOA ($0/mo) yields total monthly PITIA = $2,182.88 + $400 + $150 = $2,732.88.

4
Coverage Ratio & Inversion Thresholds

DSCR = $3,200 / $2,732.88 = 1.1709x. Maximum allowable PITIA for 1.20x coverage is $3,200 / 1.20 = $2,666.67/mo.

Frequently Asked Questions: DSCR Investor Financing

Can you get a DSCR loan with a ratio below 1.0? +
Yes. Wholesale Non-QM lenders offer 'no-ratio' or sub-1.00x DSCR programs (typically funding down to 0.75x) for properties in prime appreciation markets. These programs generally require higher down payments (25% to 30%) and cash reserves (typically 6 to 12 months PITIA).
How is DSCR calculated for residential rental properties? +
DSCR = Gross Monthly Rental Income / Total Monthly PITIA (Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance, and HOA dues). For example, $3,200 rent divided by $2,733 PITIA yields a DSCR of 1.17x.
Do DSCR loans require personal tax returns or employment verification? +
No. Under Non-QM investor underwriting guidelines, personal debt-to-income (DTI) is completely disregarded. Qualification is determined exclusively by the subject property's lease cash flow as documented by Fannie Mae Form 1007.
