Frequently Asked Questions: Bank Statement Loans

How does an underwriter calculate income on a bank statement loan? +
Underwriters review 12 or 24 consecutive months of bank statements. They tally all gross qualifying deposits, subtract non-business transfers or unusual cash spikes, and apply an expense ratio (50% standard default or a custom percentage with a CPA attestation letter). For personal checking accounts, 100% of deposits can be credited if supported by separate business account existence.
What credit score is needed for a bank statement mortgage? +
Most wholesale Non-QM bank statement lenders require a minimum credit score between 620 and 660. Borrowers with credit scores of 700+ can access loan-to-values (LTVs) up to 85%–90% with lower reserve requirements.
