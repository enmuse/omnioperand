Frequently Asked Questions: SBA Commercial Lending

What is the difference between SBA 7(a) and SBA 504 loans? +
SBA 7(a) is a versatile general-purpose loan up to $5M with variable rates (Prime + spread up to 2.75%) used for working capital, business acquisition, or equipment. SBA 504 is a fixed-rate 50/40/10 structure specifically for owner-occupied real estate and heavy machinery, pairing a 50% senior bank mortgage with a 40% government-guaranteed CDC debenture and 10% borrower cash down.
What is the minimum DSCR required for SBA loan approval? +
The SBA statutory minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) is 1.15x, meaning Net Operating Income must equal at least 115% of annual principal and interest payments. However, most commercial lenders prefer a buffer of 1.25x to 1.35x.
How much is the SBA guaranty fee in 2026? +
For loans of $150,000 or less, the fee is 0%. For loans $150,001 to $700,000, it is 2.77% of the guaranteed portion. For loans $700,001 to $1,000,000, it is 3.27%, and for loans over $1,000,000, it is 3.75%. Fees can typically be financed into the loan.
