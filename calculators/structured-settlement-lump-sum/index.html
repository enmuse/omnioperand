Frequently Asked Questions: Annuity Factoring

Why is the cash buyout offer lower than the total future payments? +
Factoring companies apply the time value of money discount rate (typically 9% to 15%) because receiving a lump sum today carries inflation risk, capital cost, and administrative/legal overhead compared to waiting 10 to 20 years for monthly installments.
Does selling a structured settlement require court approval? +
Yes. Under federal IRC §5891 and state Structured Settlement Protection Acts (SSPA), a judge must hold a formal court hearing and issue a written finding that the transfer is in the best interest of the payee and their dependents before any funds can be disbursed.
Are structured settlement cash buyouts taxable? +
If the underlying structured settlement originated from a personal physical injury or wrongful death lawsuit under IRC Section 104(a)(2), the cash payout remains 100% exempt from federal and state income taxes when processed via a Qualified Order under IRC §5891.
