Frequently Asked Questions: Business Valuation & SDE

What is the difference between SDE and EBITDA in business valuation? +
Seller's Discretionary Earnings (SDE) is used for owner-operator businesses under $5M revenue and includes the full compensation, salary, and discretionary perks of a single owner-operator. Adjusted EBITDA is used for larger businesses ($5M+ revenue) where owner compensation is replaced with a fair-market general manager salary, representing pure investor return.
What are typical SDE multiples for small businesses in 2026? +
Most healthy small businesses trade between 2.25x and 3.75x SDE. Companies with strong competitive moats, recurring contracted revenue, and low customer concentration can command 4.0x to 5.0x+ SDE.
How is a typical small business acquisition structured? +
A standard transaction structure consists of 65% to 80% Cash at Closing (often financed via an SBA 7(a) loan), 10% to 20% Seller Promissory Note (typically amortized over 3 to 5 years at 7% to 9% interest), and 10% to 20% Contingent Earnout tied to future customer retention.
