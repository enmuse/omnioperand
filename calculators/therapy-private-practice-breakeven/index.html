Frequently Asked Questions: Private Practice Economics

Why does an in-network insurance session yield significantly less than its nominal reimbursement rate? +
Nominal contracted rates are degraded by claim denial/delay rates (typically 5% to 10%), third-party clearinghouse/billing service fees (typically 5% to 8%), and an average of 15 to 30 minutes of unpaid administrative documentation and prior authorization time per session.
Can a private practice thrive with a hybrid cash and insurance model? +
Yes. Many practices maintain credentialing with 1 or 2 top-paying commercial panels (such as BCBS or Aetna) while reserving 50% to 70% of clinical calendar hours for cash-pay clients. This guarantees client acquisition stability while preventing documentation burnout.
