Frequently Asked Questions: Locum Tenens Taxes

What is required to receive non-taxable travel stipends under IRS rules? +
Under IRS Revenue Ruling 73-529, a traveling clinician must maintain a legitimate 'tax home' (permanent residence where they incur substantial ongoing living expenses) and incur duplicated lodging/meal expenses while traveling away from home for temporary clinical assignments.
When should a locum tenens physician or CRNA elect S-Corporation status? +
S-Corporation election typically becomes mathematically advantageous once net 1099 earnings exceed $150,000 to $200,000. By paying oneself an IRS 'reasonable compensation' salary and taking the remaining net profit as a corporate distribution, the provider saves 15.3% in self-employment tax on the distribution portion.
