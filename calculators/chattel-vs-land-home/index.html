The Park Lot Rent Escalation Trap

In a chattel setup, lot rent does not cease when the 20-year loan is paid off. At 3.5% annual inflation, a $650/mo lot fee grows to $917/mo by Year 10 and $1,828/mo by Year 30, paying over $400,000 in unrecoverable rent.

Asset Classification Divergence

Manufactured homes on leased land are titled as personal property (like automobiles), depreciating ~2% annually. Once permanently affixed to deeded private land, the property is taxed and appraised as titled real estate, historically appreciating 3%–4% annually.

Frequently Asked Questions: Chattel vs. Land-Home

What is a chattel loan on a manufactured home? +
A chattel loan is a personal property loan used to finance a manufactured home located on leased land (such as a mobile home park or leased private lot). Because the borrower does not own the real estate beneath the home, interest rates are typically 2% to 5% higher than real property mortgages, and amortization terms range from 15 to 23 years.
Can you convert a chattel loan to a real estate mortgage later? +
Yes. Through a process called 'conversion to real property', you can purchase deeded land, permanently affix the home to an engineered foundation, surrender the personal property title (DMV Certificate of Title), and refinance into a 30-year conventional, FHA, or VA mortgage.
