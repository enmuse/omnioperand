Intramuscular Phosphocreatine (PCr) Pool

Skeletal muscle stores approximately 120 mmol of creatine per kilogram of dry muscle. Supplementation elevates this pool by 20% to the biological ceiling of ~160 mmol/kg.

2
Loading vs Continuous Dosing Kinetics

Loading protocol: 20g/day split into 4 equal 5g doses for 5 to 7 days achieves maximal PCr saturation within 1 week. Continuous protocol: 3 to 5g/day reaches identical saturation levels at day 28.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to cycle on and off creatine? +
No. Peer-reviewed clinical safety trials spanning up to 5 continuous years show that uninterrupted creatine monohydrate supplementation does not downregulate natural creatine synthesis or harm health.
What is the best time of day to take creatine? +
Timing is secondary to consistent daily saturation. However, research suggests taking creatine post-workout alongside protein and carbohydrates slightly enhances cellular uptake via insulin-mediated transport.
