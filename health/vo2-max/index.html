Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good VO2 max for a 30-year-old? +
For a 30-year-old male, a VO2 max above 45 mL/kg/min is considered good, and above 52 is excellent. For a 30-year-old female, above 38 is good, and above 45 is excellent.
How can I increase my VO2 max? +
High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) such as Norwegian 4×4 protocols (4 minutes at 90-95% max HR followed by 3 minutes active recovery) combined with consistent Zone 2 aerobic volume produces rapid VO2 max improvements.
