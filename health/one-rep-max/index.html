Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
1RM (Epley) = Weight × (1 + Reps / 30) | 1RM (Brzycki) = Weight / (1.0278 - 0.0278 × Reps)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Epley Strength Dynamic

Formulated by Boyd Epley in 1985: 1RM = Weight × [1 + (Reps / 30)].

2
Brzycki Non-Linear Hyperbolic Form

Matt Brzycki formulation: 1RM = Weight / [1.0278 - (0.0278 × Reps)].

3
Predictive Accuracy Threshold

Both formulas maintain high correlation (r > 0.98) when repetitions are under 10. Accuracy diminishes significantly above 12 to 15 reps due to muscle endurance bias.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why estimate 1RM instead of actually testing it? +
Testing a true maximal 1RM imposes extreme neural and tendon strain with significant risk of injury. Submaximal 3-rep to 5-rep testing yields safe, highly accurate projections for program periodization.
What percentage of 1RM builds muscle best? +
Hypertrophy occurs effectively across 60% to 85% of 1RM, provided working sets are taken within 1 to 3 repetitions of muscular failure.
