Frequently Asked Questions

What is a clinical 'pack-year'? +
A pack-year is a standardized epidemiological measurement: smoking 1 pack per day for 1 year equals 1 pack-year. Smoking 2 packs per day for 10 years equals 20 pack-years.
How quickly do lungs begin to heal after quitting smoking? +
Within 2 weeks to 3 months, circulation and lung function increase up to 30%. Within 1 year, coronary heart disease risk drops to half that of a smoker. Within 10 years, lung cancer mortality risk drops by 50%.
