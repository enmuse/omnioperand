Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the US Navy body fat calculator? +
When tape measurements are taken accurately according to DoD protocols, the US Navy formula has an error margin of approximately 3% to 4% compared to clinical Dual-Energy X-ray Absorptiometry (DEXA) scans.
Where exactly should you measure your neck and waist for the Navy test? +
For men, measure neck circumference directly below the larynx (Adam's apple) and waist at the level of the navel (belly button). For women, measure neck below the larynx, waist at the point of minimal abdominal width, and hips at the widest protrusion of the buttocks.
What is the maximum body fat percentage allowed in the US Military? +
The Department of Defense enforces maximum limits: 22% to 26% for men and 33% to 36% for women depending on age bracket.
