Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Net Carbohydrate Glycemic Impact

Insoluble and soluble dietary fibers pass unabsorbed through the small intestine without triggering insulin secretion: Net Carbs = Total Carbs - Dietary Fiber.

2
Lipid Energy Balancing

Fats provide the remaining balance of caloric energy: Fat kcal = Total kcal - (Net Carbs × 4) - (Protein × 4). Fat grams = Fat kcal / 9.

3
Withrow-Woodyatt Ketogenic Index

Ketogenic Ratio = Fat grams / (Protein grams + Net Carb grams). A ratio above 1.0 indicates steady nutritional ketosis; medical epilepsy diets target 3.0 to 4.0.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between total carbs and net carbs? +
Total carbs include starches, sugars, and dietary fiber. Net carbs count only digestible carbohydrates that break down into glucose and trigger insulin.
How long does it take to enter ketosis? +
Restricting net carbs below 20-30 grams per day typically induces nutritional ketosis (blood beta-hydroxybutyrate ≥ 0.5 mmol/L) within 48 to 72 hours as hepatic glycogen stores deplete.
