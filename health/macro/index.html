Analytical Proof & Invariants

Governing Mathematical Formula:
Total Calories = (Protein × 4) + (Carbohydrates × 4) + (Fats × 9)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Caloric Density Invariants (Atwater Factors)

Proteins and carbohydrates yield exactly 4.0 kcal per gram of combustible energy. Dietary lipids (fats) yield 9.0 kcal per gram.

2
Gram Equivalence Transformation

Protein Grams = (Total kcal × Protein%) / 4. Fat Grams = (Total kcal × Fat%) / 9. Carbs Grams = (Total kcal × [100 - Protein% - Fat%]) / 4.

3
Thermic Effect of Food (TEF)

Protein has the highest thermic cost, with 20% to 30% of ingested protein calories burned during enzymatic peptide hydrolysis.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many grams of protein do I need to build muscle? +
Peer-reviewed sports nutrition literature (ISSN) establishes that 0.7 to 1.0 grams of protein per pound of body weight (1.6 to 2.2 g/kg) maximizes muscle protein synthesis (MPS).
Can dietary fats drop below 15%? +
Dropping dietary fats below 15% to 20% of total calories can impair steroid hormone synthesis (testosterone, estrogen), fat-soluble vitamin absorption (A, D, E, K), and cell membrane integrity.
