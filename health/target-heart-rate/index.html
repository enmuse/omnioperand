Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Zone 2 training so popular for endurance and longevity? +
Zone 2 training stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis, enhances cellular fat oxidation (lactate clearance), and builds capillary density in slow-twitch muscle fibers without excessive cortisol or central nervous system fatigue.
How do I accurately measure my resting heart rate? +
Measure your pulse for 60 seconds immediately upon waking in the morning while still lying quietly in bed, before drinking coffee or looking at your phone.
