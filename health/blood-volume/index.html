Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Nadler's Dimensional Hemodynamic Model

Formulated by Dr. S.B. Nadler in 1962: Men: TBV = 0.3669 × H(m)³ + 0.03219 × W(kg) + 0.6041. Women: TBV = 0.3561 × H(m)³ + 0.03308 × W(kg) + 0.1833.

2
Height-Cubed Volumetric Scaling

Because vascular tree capacity scales with three-dimensional body stature, height is cubed rather than squared, providing superior accuracy over simple body surface area (BSA) models.

3
Hematocrit Partitioning

Normal venous hematocrit partitions blood volume into ~55% liquid plasma and ~45% cellular formed elements (erythrocytes).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much blood can a person safely donate? +
A standard whole blood donation collects exactly 1 pint (~500 mL), representing roughly 8% to 10% of total circulating blood volume. The body replaces plasma fluid within 24 to 48 hours and red blood cells within 4 to 6 weeks.
What percentage of body weight is blood? +
Blood accounts for approximately 7% to 8% of an adult's total body weight (roughly 70 mL of blood per kilogram in males and 65 mL/kg in females).
