Base requirement is 0.5 fluid ounces per pound of body weight to cover respiratory, renal, and dermal insensate fluid losses.

2

Sweat rates during exercise average 0.5 to 1.0 liters per hour. Add 12 oz of water for every 30 minutes of vigorous training (0.4 oz/min).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does coffee or tea count toward my water intake? +
Yes! While caffeine has a mild diuretic effect, scientific studies demonstrate that caffeinated beverages still contribute net positive hydration toward your daily fluid balance.
Can you drink too much water? +
Yes. Drinking excessive water (e.g. several liters within an hour) without replenishing electrolytes can cause hyponatremia (water intoxication), diluting blood sodium to dangerously low levels.
