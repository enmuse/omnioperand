Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

Naegele's Obstetric Rule

Devised by Franz Naegele: EDD = LMP + 1 year - 3 months + 7 days, corresponding to exactly 280 days (40 full weeks) from LMP.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many babies are born exactly on their estimated due date? +
Only approximately 4% to 5% of babies are born on their exact calculated due date. However, over 80% of babies are born within 10 days before or after the EDD.
Why is gestational age calculated from the last period instead of conception? +
The exact date of ovulation and conception is rarely known with certainty, whereas the first day of the last menstrual period is a clear, identifiable biological marker.
