Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Dr. Devine Pharmacological Baseline

Originally developed in 1974 for renal clearance drug dosing: Men: 50.0 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet. Women: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet.

2
Robinson and Miller Empirical Adjustments

Robinson (1983) and Miller (1983) modified coefficients based on updated Metropolitan Life actuarial mortality tables.

3
Conversion to Imperial Mass

Mass in pounds = Mass (kg) × 2.20462.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do hospitals use Ideal Body Weight instead of actual weight? +
Many critical medications (aminoglycosides, anesthetics, and chemotherapy) distribute poorly into adipose tissue. Dosing based on actual weight in an obese patient could cause fatal toxicity.
What if my weight is higher than the IBW score? +
IBW formulas represent a lean baseline. Athletes with substantial skeletal muscle mass naturally exceed IBW recommendations without adverse health risks.
