Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
BAC = [Alcohol Consumed (g) / (Body Weight (g) × r)] × 100 - (β × Hours)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Standard Drink Pure Ethanol Mass

One US standard drink contains exactly 14.0 grams (0.6 fl oz) of pure ethyl alcohol.

2
Widmark Distribution Equation

BAC = [Alcohol_grams / (Weight_grams × r)] × 100 - (0.015 × Hours). Distribution ratio r = 0.68 for biological men, 0.55 for biological women (reflecting higher average body fat and lower water content).

3
Hepatic Metabolic Clearance Rate

Human liver alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH) metabolizes ethanol at a steady zero-order kinetic rate of approximately β = 0.015% BAC reduction per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can drinking coffee or taking a cold shower lower my BAC? +
No. Neither coffee, exercise, cold showers, nor vomiting accelerates alcohol elimination. The liver metabolizes alcohol at a fixed zero-order rate of ~0.015% per hour. Only time will sober you up.
What is the legal BAC limit in the United States? +
In all US states (except Utah, which is 0.05%), the legal per se limit for operating a motor vehicle is 0.08% BAC for non-commercial drivers aged 21 and older.
