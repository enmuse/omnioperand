Clinical & Statutory Telehealth Prescribing Scope

1. Ryan Haight Act Controlled Substance Safeguards (21 U.S.C. § 829(e))

Federal law prohibits dispensing controlled substances (Schedule II stimulants like Adderall/Vyvanse, Schedule IV sedatives like Xanax/Klonopin) without at least one in-person medical evaluation, unless an authorized telemedicine special registration or active federal PHE extension waiver applies.

2. State Practice Authority Variations (AANP Standards)

• Full Practice Authority (FPA: VT, ME, MA, NY, CO, WA): PMHNP independently evaluates, diagnoses, and prescribes.
• Collaborative Practice (NC, SC, GA, FL, TX): Formal registered physician supervisory or collaborative agreement required.

3. Standardized DSM-5-TR Clinical Psychometrics

• PHQ-9 (0–27): 0–4 None, 5–9 Mild, 10–14 Moderate, 15–19 Moderately Severe, 20–27 Severe.
• GAD-7 (0–21): 0–4 Minimal, 5–9 Mild, 10–14 Moderate, 15–21 Severe Anxiety.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Ryan Haight Online Pharmacy Consumer Protection Act? +
The Ryan Haight Act (21 U.S.C. § 829(e)) requires healthcare providers to conduct at least one in-person medical evaluation prior to prescribing Schedule II-V controlled substances, subject to temporary DEA telemedicine waivers.
How are PHQ-9 and GAD-7 scores interpreted? +
PHQ-9 measures depression severity: 0–4 minimal, 5–9 mild, 10–14 moderate, 15–19 moderately severe, and 20–27 severe. GAD-7 measures anxiety: 0–4 minimal, 5–9 mild, 10–14 moderate, and 15–21 severe.
When is urgent crisis protocol mandated? +
Any positive affirmative response on the Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) or PHQ-9 Question 9 indicating active intent immediately mandates 24/7 crisis protocol triage (988 Lifeline) and precludes routine asynchronous telehealth.
