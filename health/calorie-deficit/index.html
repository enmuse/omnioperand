Frequently Asked Questions

What is a safe weekly rate of fat loss? +
A sustainable rate of fat loss is 0.5% to 1.0% of your total body weight per week (typically 1 to 2 lbs per week). Faster deficits risk losing lean muscle mass and triggering metabolic slowdown.
What causes weight loss plateaus? +
As you lose weight, your BMR decreases (smaller body requires less energy), non-exercise physical activity (NEAT) unconsciously declines, and water retention may temporarily mask fat loss.
