Frequently Asked Questions

How much money does a pack-a-day smoker spend in a year? +
At $9.50 per pack, a 1-pack-a-day smoker spends $3,470 per year in direct cash outlays ($289 per month).
How much would smoking money be worth if invested for 30 years? +
Investing $3,470 annually ($289/mo) into an S&P 500 index fund compounding at 7.5% annual return grows into approximately $395,000 after 30 years.
How much life expectancy is lost from smoking one cigarette? +
Epidemiological studies indicate that every cigarette costs approximately 11 minutes of biological life expectancy. A pack-a-day habit over 10 years equates to roughly 557 days (1.5 years) of lost life.
