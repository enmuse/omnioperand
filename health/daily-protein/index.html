Frequently Asked Questions

Can the body only absorb 30 grams of protein in one meal? +
No, this is a persistent myth. Your gut absorbs virtually 100% of the amino acids you eat, even from a 100g steak; it simply digests and absorbs them over a longer timeframe of 5 to 8 hours.
Does high protein intake damage healthy kidneys? +
No. Decades of clinical trials in individuals with normal renal function show that high-protein diets (up to 1.5 g/lb) do not impair glomerular filtration rate or kidney health.
