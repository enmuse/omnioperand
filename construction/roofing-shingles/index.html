Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Roof Pitch Pythagorean Multiplier

The pitch factor secant: M_pitch = √[1 + (pitch / 12)²]. For a 6/12 pitch, M_pitch = √(1 + 0.25) = 1.118.

2
True Geometric Slope Area

True Area = Footprint × M_pitch. Order Area = True Area · (1 + waste/100).

3
Roofing Square and Bundle Quantization

One roofing square = 100 square feet. Architectural shingles pack 3 bundles per square: Bundles = Squares × 3.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 'square' in roofing? +
A roofing square is a standard unit of measurement equal to exactly 100 square feet of roof surface.
How many bundles of shingles make a square? +
For almost all standard fiberglass and architectural shingles, exactly 3 bundles cover 1 square (100 sq ft). Heavy designer shingles may require 4 or 5 bundles per square.
