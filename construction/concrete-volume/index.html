Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
V = Length · Width · Depth · (1 + Waste%) / 27 yd³ | V_cyl = π · (d/2)² · Height

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Slab Cubic Footage Calculation

Convert thickness to feet: Depth (ft) = Depth (in) / 12. Volume = Length (ft) × Width (ft) × Depth (ft) × Quantity.

2
2. Cubic Yard Conversion & Waste Factor

One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet. V_yd³ = Volume_cuft / 27. Multiply by (1 + Waste% / 100) to account for subgrade settling, excavation irregularities, and wheelbarrow spillage.

3
3. Commercial Premix Bag Equivalents

Standard 80-lb bags yield approximately 0.60 cu ft of mixed concrete (45 bags per cu yd). 60-lb bags yield approximately 0.45 cu ft (60 bags per cu yd).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 80lb bags of concrete do I need for a 10x10 slab at 4 inches thick? +
A 10x10 ft slab at 4 inches depth is 33.33 cubic feet (1.23 cubic yards). With a 10% waste buffer (1.36 cu yd / 36.67 cu ft), you need exactly 62 bags of 80-lb concrete, or 82 bags of 60-lb concrete.
Why is a 10% concrete waste buffer recommended by contractors? +
Soil subgrades are rarely laser-flat; settling, bowing forms, irregular trench depths, and residue inside mixing equipment consistently consume 5% to 10% more concrete than theoretical dimensions.
When is ready-mix truck delivery cheaper than bags? +
If your project requires more than 1.5 to 2.0 cubic yards (approximately 90+ 80lb bags), ordering bulk ready-mix delivery by truck is significantly cheaper, faster, and structurally more homogeneous.
