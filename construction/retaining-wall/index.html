Governing Mathematical Formula:
Blocks = [Wall Length (ft) × Wall Height (ft)] / Block Face Area (sq ft)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Buried Base Course Consideration

Retaining walls require 1 course buried beneath finished grade for stability: Total Course Height = Height + (block_height / 12).

2
Block Face Yield

Block face area A_b = (block_length × block_height) / 144 sq ft. Total Blocks = ceil[(Wall Length × Total Height) / A_b] × 1.05.

3
Hydrostatic Drainage Calculus

Retaining walls fail due to water pressure. A 12-inch clear gravel drainage chimney is placed behind the wall: V_gravel = Length × Height × 1.0 ft.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does a retaining wall require an engineer's stamp? +
Most municipal building codes mandate a stamped civil engineering design for any retaining wall with an exposed height of 4 feet or higher (measured from bottom of footing).
What is geogrid and when is it necessary? +
Geogrid is a high-tensile polyester mesh embedded between block courses extending back into compacted soil to reinforce walls over 3 to 4 feet in height.
