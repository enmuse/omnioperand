Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Generator Size = Total Running Watts + Largest Motor Surge Watts

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Inductive Motor Inrush Current

Compressors and pumps require 2 to 3 times their running power during the first 2 to 3 seconds of startup to establish magnetic rotor fields.

2
Simultaneous Surge Rule

Calculate running load by summing all continuous devices. Then add only the SINGLE largest motor startup surge, since probability of multiple motors starting on the exact same millisecond is near zero.

3
Safety Operating Headroom

Generators should not operate continuously at 100% capacity. Add a 20% safety margin: Generator kW = Peak Load × 1.20.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a portable generator power a central air conditioner? +
A typical 3-ton central AC requires 3,500 running watts but 10,000+ starting watts. To run on a portable generator under 10 kW, you must install an inductive soft-start kit on the AC compressor.
What is the difference between an inverter generator and open-frame generator? +
Inverter generators produce clean pure sine wave power (<3% Total Harmonic Distortion) safe for sensitive computers and TVs, and throttle engine speed to match electrical demand, saving fuel.
