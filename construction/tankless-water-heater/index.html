Frequently Asked Questions

Can an electric tankless water heater supply a whole house in cold climates? +
In northern regions where winter groundwater is 35°F to 45°F, electric tankless units struggle to provide more than 3 to 4 GPM unless the home has a massive 300A or 400A electrical service panel.
What size gas line is needed for a gas tankless water heater? +
High-output gas tankless units (199,000 BTU) typically require a dedicated 3/4-inch gas line directly from the meter rather than the 1/2-inch line used by conventional tank heaters.
