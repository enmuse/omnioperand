Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Trench Volumetric Formulation

Gross excavation volume equals Length × Width × Depth in cubic feet, divided by 27 to convert to cubic yards.

2
2. Cylindrical Pipe Displacement Deduction

Each pipe displaces volume equal to π × (OD/2)² × L in cubic feet. Subtracting total pipe volume avoids over-ordering expensive ready-mix slurry.

3
3. ACI 229R Mix Proportions

Standard excavatable flowable fill utilizes a low-cement mix: ~50–100 lbs cement, 250–300 lbs fly ash, ~2,600 lbs sand, and ~450 lbs water per cubic yard to maintain compressive strength under 100 psi for easy future re-excavation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is flowable fill (CLSM)? +
Controlled Low-Strength Material (CLSM), commonly known as flowable fill or slurry, is a self-leveling, self-compacting cementitious backfill material used instead of compacted soil in utility trenches.
Why is flowable fill limited to under 100 psi for utility trenches? +
Keeping compressive strength below 100 psi allows maintenance crews to easily dig through the material in the future using standard hand shovels or backhoes without jackhammers.
Does flowable fill require manual compaction or vibrating plates? +
No. Flowable fill flows into all voids around pipes under gravity, requiring zero mechanical compaction and eliminating trench settlement.
