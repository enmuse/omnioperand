Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Type N and Type S mortar? +
Type N mortar (medium strength, ~750 psi) is ideal for exterior above-grade brick veneer. Type S mortar (high strength, ~1,800 psi) is required for below-grade foundations or structural walls.
How many bricks come on a standard delivery cube/pallet? +
A standard brick pallet (cube) contains 500 to 525 modular bricks and weighs approximately 2,000 to 2,400 lbs.
