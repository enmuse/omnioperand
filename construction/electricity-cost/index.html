Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Kilowatt-Hour Integral

Energy consumption in kilowatt-hours: kWh_daily = (Watts × Daily Hours) / 1,000.

2
Monthly and Annual Scaling

Monthly kWh = kWh_daily × 30. Annual kWh = kWh_daily × 365.

3
Monetary Utility Assessment

Cost = Total kWh × Utility Rate ($/kWh).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much electricity does a space heater use? +
A standard 1,500-watt space heater running 8 hours a day consumes 12 kWh per day, costing approximately $1.92 per day or $57.60 per month at $0.16/kWh.
What household appliances use the most electricity? +
Central air conditioning and heat pumps, electric water heaters, electric clothes dryers, and EV chargers consume the vast majority of residential power.
