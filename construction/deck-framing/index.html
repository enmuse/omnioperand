Governing Mathematical Formula:
Joist Count = ceil[Deck Width (in) / On-Center Spacing (in)] + 1

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Joist Layout Algorithm

Joists run perpendicular to the ledger board. Number of joists = ceil[(Deck Length × 12) / Spacing] + 1 (for end rim joist).

2
Decking Board Quantity

Boards run parallel to the house across joists. Effective board width with 1/8" gap = 5.625". Boards = ceil[(Deck Projection × 12) / 5.625].

3
Beam Post Support Calculation

Drop beams require 6×6 support posts spaced no more than 8 feet apart under standard 50 psf total load.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 12-inch joist spacing needed for composite boards? +
Composite decking (Trex, TimberTech) has higher flexibility than natural lumber. At 16" spacing, composite boards will sag or feel bouncy under summer sun.
What fasteners should be used with pressure-treated lumber? +
Copper preservative chemicals (ACQ, MCQ) in modern pressure-treated wood corrode standard steel. Use hot-dipped galvanized or 304/316 grade stainless steel screws.
