Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Cubic Yards = [Length (ft) × Width (ft) × (Thickness (in) / 12)] / 27

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Dimensional Volumetric Conversion

Convert slab thickness from inches to feet: h_ft = thickness / 12. Volume in cubic feet is V_cf = L × W × h_ft.

2
Cubic Yards Derivation

One cubic yard contains 3 × 3 × 3 = 27 cubic feet: V_cy = V_cf / 27.

3
Waste Adjustment and Bag Factorization

Total ordered volume includes waste factor: V_total = V_cy · (1 + waste/100). One 80lb bag yields ~0.60 cu ft, and one 60lb bag yields ~0.45 cu ft.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I order ready-mix truck delivery vs buying bags? +
Generally, if your project requires more than 1 cubic yard (approx. 45 to 60 bags of 80lb concrete), ordering a ready-mix concrete delivery truck is significantly faster, more consistent, and less labor-intensive.
What thickness should a patio slab be? +
A standard residential concrete patio or walkway should be 4 inches thick. For vehicle parking or heavy equipment, 5 to 6 inches with reinforcement rebar is recommended.
