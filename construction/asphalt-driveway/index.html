Governing Mathematical Formula:
Asphalt Tons = [Area (sq ft) × Depth (in) × 110 lbs/sq yd/in] / 2,000

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Square Yardage Transformation

Area in square yards: A_sy = (Length × Width) / 9.

2
Asphalt Paving Density Constant

Compact hot mix asphalt weighs approximately 110 lbs per square yard per inch of thickness (145 lbs/cu ft).

3
Tonnage Derivation

Tons = (A_sy × Depth_in × 110) / 2000. Add 5% for edge tamping compaction margin.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum recommended asphalt thickness for a home driveway? +
A residential driveway requires a minimum of 2.5 to 3 inches of compacted asphalt over a 6 to 8 inch compacted aggregate base (crush-and-run).
How long after paving can I park cars on new asphalt? +
You can walk on new asphalt within 24 hours, but wait 3 to 5 full days before parking passenger vehicles, and avoid turning wheels while stationary.
