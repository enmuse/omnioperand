Governing Mathematical Formula:
N_bars = [ (Span - 2·Clearance) / Spacing ] + 1 | Weight = Total Linear Feet · (lb/ft)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Effective Grid Spans

Subtract edge clearance from both sides: Clear Length = Length - 2·(Clearance/12), Clear Width = Width - 2·(Clearance/12).

2
2. Bar Counts Along Axes

Number of lengthwise bars = floor(Clear Width · 12 / Spacing) + 1. Number of crosswise bars = floor(Clear Length · 12 / Spacing) + 1.

3
3. Splice Overlap & Unit Weights

ACI 318 mandates a minimum lap splice of 30 to 40 bar diameters (typically 15 to 20 inches for #4 rebar). Add a 10% overlap allowance to total linear footage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size rebar should be used for a 4-inch concrete slab? +
#4 rebar (1/2-inch nominal diameter, weighing 0.668 lbs per linear foot) spaced at 12 or 18 inches on center is the engineering gold standard for residential slabs and driveways.
Why is 3 inches of edge clearance required for rebar? +
Concrete cover protects carbon steel rebar from water permeation and oxidation (rust). ACI 318 requires 3 inches of cover when concrete is poured directly against earth.
How much tie wire do I need per ton of rebar? +
On average, tie wire usage is approximately 15 to 20 pounds of 16-gauge tie wire per ton of rebar, or roughly one 3-to-4 lb roll per 1,000 grid intersections.
