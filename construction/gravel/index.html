Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should a gravel driveway be? +
A proper gravel driveway consists of 8 to 12 inches total: 4 to 6 inches of coarse #3 or #4 ballast base stone, followed by 4 inches of crushed stone (#57 or ABC crush-and-run).
How many tons of gravel fit in a tri-axle dump truck? +
A standard tri-axle dump truck typically hauls 18 to 22 tons of aggregate, while a standard tandem dump truck carries 12 to 14 tons.
