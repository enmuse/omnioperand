Thermal Resistance Definition

R-value measures conductive heat flow resistance: R = ΔT / (Heat Flux Q). Higher R-values indicate greater thermal insulation.

2
Fiberglass Thickness Equivalence

Frequently Asked Questions

What R-value is recommended for an attic? +
The US Department of Energy and International Energy Conservation Code (IECC) recommend R-38 to R-49 for moderate climates, and R-60 for cold northern zones.
Can I install new insulation directly over old insulation? +
Yes, as long as the old insulation is dry and free of mold or rodent contamination. Never use kraft-faced (vapor barrier) insulation on top of existing insulation; use unfaced batts or loose-fill blow-in.
