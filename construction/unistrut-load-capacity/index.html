Governing Mathematical Formula:
Uniform: W = (8 · S · σ_allow) / L | Deflection Δ = (5 · W · L³) / (384 · E · I) | σ_allow = 25,000 psi

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Flexural Bending Strength Capacity

MFMA-4 specifies allowable bending stress of 25,000 psi for cold-formed steel strut channels. For uniform load, maximum moment is M = W · L / 8 = S · σ_allow, yielding W = (8 · S · 25,000) / L.

2
2. Elastic Deflection Formulation

For modulus E = 29,500,000 psi, center deflection under total uniform load W is Δ = (5 · W · L³) / (384 · E · I). For center point load P, deflection is Δ = (P · L³) / (48 · E · I).

3
3. Dual Failure Mode Comparison

Long spans are governed by serviceability deflection limits (L/240 or L/360) rather than structural yield strength.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can a 10-foot Unistrut hold? +
A standard 12-gauge P1000 Unistrut over a 10-foot (120-inch) span can support approximately 280 lbs of uniformly distributed load before exceeding stress limits, but deflection will be ~1.0 inch.
What is the strongest Unistrut profile? +
P1001 (double back-to-back 12-gauge welded channel) and P5000 (3-1/4" deep single channel) provide over 4 to 5 times the load capacity of standard P1000.
What is the allowable stress for Unistrut? +
The Metal Framing Manufacturers Association (MFMA) standards specify an allowable design bending stress of 25,000 psi based on a 1.67 safety factor on 33,000 psi yield cold-formed steel.
