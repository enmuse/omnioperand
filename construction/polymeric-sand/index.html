Analytical Derivation & Methodology

1
1. Paver Unit Area & Joint Ratio

The effective footprint of a paver including its surrounding joint width is (L + W_j) × (W + W_j). The ratio of solid paver face area to total footprint establishes the joint surface percentage: Joint% = 1 - (L × W) / [(L + W_j) × (W + W_j)].

2
2. Joint Volume & Compaction Allowance

Multiplying the patio area by Joint% and effective joint depth gives raw joint volume: V_raw = Area × Joint% × (D_j / 12). Applying the ICPI 10% compaction buffer yields design volume: V_design = V_raw × (1 + Waste%).

3
3. Commercial Bag Weight & Bedding Base

Commercial polymeric sand has an installed bulk density of approx 100 lbs/cu ft (1,600 kg/m³). Dividing total weight by 50 lbs gives required bags. The underlying 1-inch (25 mm) ASTM C33 concrete sand bedding layer is computed independently: V_bed = (Area × 1/12) / 27 × 1.10 cubic yards.
