Governing Mathematical Formula:
Sheets = (Total Wall Area + Ceiling Area) / Sheet Area

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Total Enclosing Envelope Area

Wall area = 2 × (L + W) × H. Ceiling area = L × W. Total area = Wall area + Ceiling area.

2
Sheet Yield and Waste Factor

With a standard 10% cutting waste factor, Sheets = ceil[(Total Area × 1.10) / Sheet Size].

3
Fasteners and Mud Consumables

Drywall sheets require approximately 32 screws per 4×8 panel (~12" edge and 16" field spacing). Mud consumes ~0.053 gallons per square foot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use 4×8 or 4×12 drywall panels? +
4×12 panels produce 33% fewer butt joints, resulting in faster taping and smoother finishes. However, 4×12 panels are heavier (~75 lbs) and require two people to lift.
Should drywall be installed horizontally or vertically on walls? +
In residential construction, hanging panels horizontally across studs increases shear strength and places seams at a convenient 4-foot height for taping.
