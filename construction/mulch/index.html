Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Cubic Yards = [Bed Area (sq ft) × Depth (in)] / 324

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Dimensional Divisor Derivation

One cubic yard = 27 cu ft = 27 × 12 = 324 board-feet. Therefore, 1 cubic yard covers exactly 324 square feet at 1 inch depth.

2
Cubic Yardage Formula

Cubic Yards = (Area × Depth_inches) / 324.

3
Retail Bag Equivalence

Since 1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet: 2 cu ft bags = ceil(cuft / 2), and 3 cu ft bags = ceil(cuft / 3).

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should mulch be applied? +
A 3-inch depth is optimal for suppressing weeds and retaining soil moisture. Mulch deeper than 4 inches can starve plant roots of oxygen and create fungal buildup.
When is bulk mulch cheaper than bags? +
Bulk delivery is typically economical when you need 3 or more cubic yards (approx. 40+ bags). Under 2 cubic yards, retail bagged mulch is often more convenient.
