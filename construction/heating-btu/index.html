Frequently Asked Questions

What does AFUE stand for on a furnace? +
AFUE stands for Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency. A 96% AFUE rating means 96% of the fuel is converted into usable heat, while only 4% is lost through chimney exhaust.
What happens if a furnace is too big for the house? +
An oversized furnace will heat the house too rapidly and shut off before circulating heat evenly, resulting in extreme temperature swings between rooms and excessive wear on heat exchangers.
