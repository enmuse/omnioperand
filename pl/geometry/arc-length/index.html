Wyprowadzenie Matematyczne Krok po Kroku

1
Radian Definition and Arc Length

One radian is defined as the central angle subtending an arc equal in length to the circle's radius. Converting degrees to radians: θ_rad = θ° · (π / 180°). Arc Length s = r · θ_rad.

2
Sector Area Derivation

Sector area is proportional to the fraction of the full circle: Area_sector = (θ° / 360°) · πr² = ½ · r² · θ_rad.

3
Chord Length and Segment Area

Bisecting the isosceles triangle formed by the radius arms yields chord length: c = 2r · sin(θ / 2). Circular Segment Area = Area_sector - Area_triangle = ½ · r² · (θ_rad - sin θ_rad).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a circular sector and a circular segment? +
A circular sector is a pie-slice shape bounded by two radius lines and an arc. A circular segment is the smaller region between a straight chord line and the circular arc.
Why are angles measured in radians in calculus? +
In calculus, using radians preserves simple derivatives such as d/dx(sin x) = cos x. If measured in degrees, an extra unwieldy factor of π/180 appears in every trigonometric derivative and integral.
