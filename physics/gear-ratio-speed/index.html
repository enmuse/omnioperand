Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Speed (MPH) = (Engine RPM × Tire Diameter in inches) / (Trans Ratio × Axle Ratio × 336)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Total Drivetrain Ratio

Overall Gear Ratio R_total = Transmission Ratio × Axle Final Drive Ratio. For example: 0.68 × 3.73 = 2.536:1.

2
Wheel Rotational Frequency

Wheel RPM = Engine RPM / R_total.

3
Road Linear Velocity Constant

Speed MPH = (Wheel RPM × π × Diameter × 60) / 63,360 = (Engine RPM × Diameter) / (R_total × 336.135).

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the 336 constant come from in the gear ratio formula? +
The constant derives from converting units: 1 mile = 63,360 inches, 1 hour = 60 minutes, and circumference = π × D: 63,360 / (60 × π) = 336.135.
How does changing axle gear ratio from 3.23 to 4.10 affect cruising RPM? +
Installing shorter (numerically higher) 4.10 gears increases wheel torque and acceleration by ~27%, but raises highway engine RPM at any given speed by 27%, increasing cabin drone and fuel consumption.
