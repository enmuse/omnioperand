Frequently Asked Questions

Why is tongue weight so critical for trailer towing? +
If tongue weight is under 10%, the trailer becomes back-heavy, inducing violent aerodynamic fishtailing and sway at highway speeds that can flip both truck and trailer. If tongue weight exceeds 15%, the rear truck axle is overloaded, unloading front steer tires and compromising braking.
What is the difference between GVWR and GCWR? +
GVWR (Gross Vehicle Weight Rating) is the maximum legal weight of the tow vehicle alone (including tongue weight). GCWR (Gross Combined Weight Rating) is the maximum legal weight of truck AND trailer combined.
