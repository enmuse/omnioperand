Frequently Asked Questions

What size roll of vinyl wrap do I need for a full car wrap? +
For compacts and sports coupes, a 50-foot × 5-foot roll is usually sufficient. For mid-size sedans and crossovers, buy a 60-foot × 5-foot roll (300 sq ft). Full-size trucks and large SUVs require 75 feet.
What is the difference between Cast and Calendered vinyl? +
Always use Cast vinyl (3M 2080, Avery Dennison SW900) for vehicles! Cast vinyl has no memory and will not shrink, peel, or pop out of deep body contours like cheap calendered film.
