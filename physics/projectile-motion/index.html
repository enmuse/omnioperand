Governing Mathematical Formula:
Range R = [v₀² · sin(2θ)] / g | Max Height H = [v₀² · sin²(θ)] / (2g)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Orthogonal Vector Velocity Decomposition

Horizontal velocity: v_x = v₀ · cos(θ). Vertical velocity: v_y = v₀ · sin(θ). In vacuum kinematics, horizontal velocity remains constant (a_x = 0).

2
Apogee Altitude and Vertical Ascent Time

At maximum height, vertical velocity drops to zero (v_y = 0): t_apex = (v₀ sin θ) / g. Peak height H = y₀ + (v₀² sin² θ) / 2g.

3
Quadratic Ground Impact Time

Solving y(t) = y₀ + (v₀ sin θ)t - ½gt² = 0 for positive root t_impact yields total flight duration. Range R = v_x · t_impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 45 degrees the optimal launch angle for maximum range? +
Because horizontal range is proportional to sin(2θ). The sine function achieves its maximum value of 1.0 at 90°, so 2θ = 90° implies θ = 45° in an ideal vacuum without aerodynamic drag.
How does air resistance change the optimal launch angle? +
Aerodynamic atmospheric drag dissipates kinetic energy continuously over distance, shifting the optimal launch angle for baseballs, golf balls, and artillery down to 32° to 38°.
