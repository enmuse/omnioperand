Frequently Asked Questions

Does towing a trailer reduce truck payload capacity? +
Yes! Trailer tongue weight rests directly on the truck hitch ball and must be subtracted pound-for-pound from available payload capacity. An 800 lb tongue weight consumes 800 lbs of your truck's payload capacity.
Can helper springs or air bags increase payload capacity? +
No! Air bags and helper springs level the vehicle and prevent rear suspension sag, but they do NOT legally or mechanically increase the manufacturer GVWR, which is limited by axles, wheel bearings, frames, and brakes.
