Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Quadratic Velocity Dependence

Aerodynamic drag scales with the square of velocity (v²). Doubling road speed quadruples total drag force.

2
2. Cubic Power Law

Power is force multiplied by velocity: Power = F_drag × v = ½ · ρ · v³ · C_d · A. Doubling speed requires eight times (2³ = 8) as much engine horsepower to overcome wind resistance.

3
3. Altitude & Temperature Air Density Corrections

Air density ρ decreases with elevation and higher temperatures according to the ideal gas law: ρ = P / (R_spec · T). Standard sea level air is 0.0765 lb/ft³ (1.225 kg/m³).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does highway speed dramatically reduce fuel efficiency and EV range? +
Because of the cubic power law: overcoming aerodynamic drag at 80 mph requires ~50% more horsepower than at 65 mph (80³ / 65³ ≈ 1.86 ratio of aerodynamic power).
What is a good drag coefficient (Cd) for modern cars? +
Sleek modern electric vehicles achieve Cd between 0.20 and 0.24. Typical consumer sedans achieve 0.26 to 0.30, while pickup trucks and SUVs range from 0.35 to 0.45.
How is frontal area measured? +
Frontal area is approximately 85% of total vehicle width multiplied by total vehicle height (A ≈ 0.85 × W × H).
