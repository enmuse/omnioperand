Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Runtime (Hours) = (Battery Volts × Amp Hours × DoD %) / (Inverter Load Watts / Inverter Efficiency)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Total Stored Energy

Watt-Hours Wh = Voltage × Amp-Hours. For a 12V 200Ah bank: 12 × 200 = 2,400 Wh.

2
Depth of Discharge and Usable Energy

Usable Energy = Total Wh × (DoD% / 100). Lead-acid batteries degrade rapidly if discharged past 50%; LiFePO4 lithium batteries can be discharged to 85-90%.

3
Inverter Inefficiency Loss

DC-to-AC inverters have 85-90% conversion efficiency: Real DC Load = AC Load / 0.88. Runtime = Usable Wh / Real DC Load.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should lead-acid batteries not be discharged below 50%? +
Discharging lead-acid (flooded or AGM) batteries past 50% causes irreversible lead-sulfate crystallization on the plates, slashing battery lifespan from 1,000 cycles down to under 250 cycles.
What size inverter do I need for a 1,000-watt appliance? +
Choose an inverter rated for at least 1,200 to 1,500 watts continuous to handle startup surge spikes and maintain cool operating temperatures.
