Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

Wheel Horsepower Derivation

WHP = Crank HP × Drivetrain Transfer Efficiency (manual RWD: 15% loss; AWD: 20-22% loss).

2
Hale and Fox Empirical Dynamic Laws

Elapsed Time ET = 5.825 × (Weight / HP)^(1/3). Trap Speed = 234 × (HP / Weight)^(1/3).

3
Tire Traction and 60-Foot Sensitivity

These formulas assume optimal launch traction on a prepped NHRA drag strip surface. Street tires on unprepped asphalt typically add 0.3 to 0.6 seconds to ET.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are drag racing quarter-mile formulas? +
The Fox and Hale formulas are remarkably accurate (typically within ±0.2 seconds) for typical passenger and sports cars with competent drivers on drag radial or slick tires.
Why does reducing vehicle weight lower ET faster than adding power? +
Reducing weight improves every phase of vehicle physics simultaneously: launch grip, 60-foot sprint, braking, and rotational tire acceleration without adding heat or drivetrain stress.
