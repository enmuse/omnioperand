Metric to Imperial Tire Geometry

Rolling Circumference and Revolutions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum safe tire size change without recalibration? +
Automotive engineers recommend keeping overall tire diameter changes within ±3.0% of factory specifications to prevent anti-lock brake (ABS), traction control, and speedometer calibration malfunctions.
Will larger tires make my car faster or slower? +
Larger diameter tires increase your top speed at a given engine RPM, but decrease off-the-line wheel torque and acceleration because they effectively create a taller final drive gear ratio.
