Drude Electron Conduction Model

Discovered experimentally by Georg Ohm in 1827: Current I through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to voltage V and inversely proportional to resistance R: I = V / R.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between AC impedance and DC resistance? +
In direct current (DC) circuits, resistance R opposes electron flow. In alternating current (AC) circuits, total opposition is termed impedance Z = √(R² + X²), where reactance X accounts for inductive coils and capacitors.
Why does doubling voltage quadruple power in a fixed resistance? +
Because power scales with voltage squared: P = V² / R. If voltage across a heating element doubles from 120V to 240V, heat output increases by a factor of 4.
