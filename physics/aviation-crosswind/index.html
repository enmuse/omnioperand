Governing Mathematical Formula:
Crosswind = Wind Speed × sin(Wind Angle - Runway Heading) | Headwind = Wind Speed × cos(Wind Angle - Runway Heading)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Relative Angular Delta

Angular Offset θ = |Wind Direction - Runway Heading|.

2
Trigonometric Vector Decomposition

Crosswind = Wind Speed × sin(θ). Headwind = Wind Speed × cos(θ).

3
Tailwind Safety Hazard

If θ > 90°, cos(θ) becomes negative, representing a dangerous tailwind that dramatically increases landing rollout distance and stall risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between maximum demonstrated crosswind and an operating limitation? +
For FAA Part 23 general aviation aircraft, maximum demonstrated crosswind is not an airworthiness limitation; it is simply the maximum crosswind tested by the factory test pilot during certification. However, exceeding it during an accident invites FAA negligence enforcement.
How do pilots correct for crosswinds on landing? +
Pilots utilize either the crab technique (heading pointed into wind until flare) or the wing-low slip technique (aileron into wind to prevent drift, opposite rudder to align fuselage with runway centerline).
