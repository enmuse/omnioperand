Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Full Charge Cost = Battery Capacity (kWh) × Electricity Rate ($/kWh) / Charging Efficiency

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
AC Charging Efficiency Adjustment

AC home charging incurs 10% thermal and inverter energy loss (efficiency η ≈ 90%). Energy drawn from grid = Battery Capacity / 0.90.

2
Direct Full Recharge Cost

Cost_home = (Battery kWh / 0.90) × Rate_home. Cost_public = (Battery kWh / 0.90) × Rate_public.

3
Distance Energy Intensity (Wh/mi)

Efficiency = (Battery kWh × 1000) / Range Miles. Cost per Mile = Cost_home / Range Miles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to charge a Tesla at home? +
For a standard 75 kWh battery pack at the US average residential electricity rate of $0.16/kWh, a full charge costs approximately $13.30, providing roughly 300 miles of range ($0.044 per mile).
Is DC fast charging more expensive than gasoline? +
DC fast charging ($0.40-$0.50/kWh) works out to roughly $0.10 to $0.13 per mile, which is still cheaper than a 25 MPG gas car ($0.14/mile at $3.50/gal), but roughly 3× more expensive than charging overnight at home.
