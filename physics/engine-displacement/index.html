Governing Mathematical Formula:
Displacement = π × (Bore / 2)² × Stroke × Cylinders

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Cylinder Swept Volume Geometry

Swept Volume V_cyl = π × r² × h = π × (Bore / 2)² × Stroke.

2
Total Multi-Cylinder Displacement

Total Displacement = V_cyl × Number of Cylinders. Convert mm³ to cm³ (cc) by dividing by 1,000.

3
Bore / Stroke Mechanical Classification

Bore / Stroke > 1.0 = Over-square (high-revving racing engines); = 1.0 = Square engine (balanced torque/revs); < 1.0 = Under-square (long-stroke low-end diesel torque).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a square and over-square engine? +
An over-square engine has a bore larger than its stroke, permitting larger intake valves and higher safe redline RPMs (e.g. Ferrari, Formula 1). An under-square engine has a longer stroke, favoring low-RPM torque for towing and trucks.
How do you convert cc to cubic inches? +
Multiply cubic centimeters by 0.0610237, or divide by 16.387. For example: 5,000 cc × 0.0610237 = 305 CID (Chevy 305).
