A la Carte Unbundled Value

2
Consumer Promotional Discount

3
Unit Profit Economics

Bundle Gross Profit = $65.00 - $20.00 COGS = $45.00. Gross Margin % = ($45.00 / $65.00) × 100 = 69.2%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do bundles improve marketing efficiency? +
Bundles immediately raise Average Order Value (AOV). Because shipping a two-item box costs almost the same as shipping a single item, bundling leverages fixed shipping and ad acquisition costs across more gross margin dollars.
What is the recommended discount percentage for bundles? +
Bundles typically perform best with a discount between 15% and 22%. Lower discounts fail to convert hesitant shoppers, while discounts above 25% unnecessarily erode contribution margins.
