Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
GMROI = Gross Margin $ / Average Inventory Cost = (Gross Margin % × Sales Revenue) / Average Inventory Cost

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Gross Profit Dollar Generation

Gross Margin $ = Revenue × Margin % = $1,200,000 × 55.0% = $660,000 total gross profit.

2
GMROI Ratio Calculation

GMROI = Gross Margin $ / Average Inventory Cost = $660,000 / $200,000 = $3.30 per dollar invested (330%).

3
Turnover Breakdown Relationship

COGS = $1,200,000 × (1 - 0.55) = $540,000. Inventory Turns = $540,000 / $200,000 = 2.70 turns. GMROI = 2.70 turns × [55% / (1 - 55%)] = $3.30.

4
Capital Productivity Evaluation

For every $1.00 held in warehouse inventory, the brand extracts $3.30 in gross margin annually.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good GMROI in retail merchandising? +
A GMROI of 1.0 ($1.00) means the inventory breaks even on cost. Retailers generally target a GMROI of at least $2.00 to cover operating overhead. Elite e-commerce brands achieve GMROIs between $3.00 and $4.50.
How does GMROI differ from Gross Margin %? +
A high gross margin percentage on an item that takes 2 years to sell produces terrible cash flow. GMROI balances both profitability (margin %) and speed (inventory turnover).
