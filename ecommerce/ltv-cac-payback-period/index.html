Frequently Asked Questions

What is the gold standard LTV:CAC ratio for venture and bootstrap e-commerce? +
A healthy benchmark is 3:1. Ratios below 2:1 indicate unprofitable customer acquisition. Ratios above 5:1 signal that the brand is under-investing in growth and leaving market share on the table.
Why should LTV be calculated on gross profit rather than top-line revenue? +
Calculating LTV on revenue is a dangerous vanity metric. You cannot spend revenue to pay acquisition costs; only gross profit margin dollars cover ad spend and operating overhead.
