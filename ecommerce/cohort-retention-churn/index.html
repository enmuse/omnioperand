Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a healthy retention curve in e-commerce? +
A healthy retention curve asymptotically flattens rather than decaying to zero. If the curve flattens between 15% and 25%, the business has a viable repeat customer base capable of compounding.
How does subscription box retention differ from non-subscription e-commerce? +
Subscription brands generally expect 60-70% month-over-month retention. Non-subscription e-commerce measures repeat purchase rate, where 25-35% within 12 months is considered excellent.
