Frequently Asked Questions

Why use tiered affiliate commissions instead of flat rates? +
Tiered commissions gamify affiliate recruitment, incentivizing top creators and review sites to grant premium homepage placement to cross the higher-paying volume threshold.
What is an affiliate network override fee? +
Affiliate tracking networks (like Impact.com, Rakuten, CJ) charge the merchant a software fee—usually 15-20% of the commission paid, or 1-3% of tracked revenue—for tracking pixel attribution and fraud detection.
