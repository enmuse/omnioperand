Frequently Asked Questions

Why shouldn't I offer free shipping on all orders without a threshold? +
Unconditional free shipping destroys profitability on low-ticket orders where shipping costs can consume 30% to 50% of the entire purchase value. A calculated threshold forces customers to bundle items, expanding gross profit to subsidize shipping.
What is the psychological sweet spot for a free shipping threshold? +
Consumer psychological testing demonstrates that setting the threshold 15% to 30% above your current AOV produces maximum conversion lift without creating an insurmountable cart addition barrier.
