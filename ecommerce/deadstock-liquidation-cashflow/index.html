Frequently Asked Questions

What is deadstock carrying cost? +
Carrying cost is the complete expense of holding inventory: warehouse rent, utilities, physical handling, insurance, property tax, shrinkage risk, and the opportunity cost of having working capital locked up.
Why do retailers accept 30-40 cents on the dollar for deadstock? +
Holding deadstock for another year consumes 20-30% of its value in warehousing fees alone, after which the item may be completely obsolete. Liquidating immediately redeploys cash into high-velocity bestsellers.
