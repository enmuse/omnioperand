Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between standard-size and oversize on Amazon FBA? +
Standard size items are under 20 lbs and within 18 × 14 × 8 inches. Any item exceeding any of these thresholds moves into oversized tiers, where FBA fulfillment fees jump significantly.
Does Amazon refund referral fees when a customer returns an item? +
Amazon refunds 80% of the referral fee upon return, retaining an administrative fee of 20% of the original referral fee (up to $5.00).
