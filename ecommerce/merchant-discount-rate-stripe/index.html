Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my effective MDR higher than the advertised 2.9% rate? +
The fixed $0.30 per transaction fee causes effective rates to climb significantly on lower ticket sizes. On a $10 transaction, $0.30 represents an extra 3.0%, making the effective fee 5.9%.
When does it make sense to switch to Interchange-Plus pricing? +
When processing volume exceeds $50,000 to $80,000 per month, switching from flat-rate (2.9% + 30¢) to Interchange-Plus (typically wholesale interchange + 0.15% to 0.30% + 10¢) can reduce processing costs by 20% to 35%.
