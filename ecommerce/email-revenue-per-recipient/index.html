Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good Revenue Per Recipient (RPR) for e-commerce broadcasts? +
General promotional email blasts typically achieve an RPR between $0.10 and $0.25. High-intent automated flows (abandoned cart, post-purchase replenishment) frequently achieve RPRs between $1.50 and $4.00.
How did Apple's Mail Privacy Protection (MPP) impact open rate tracking? +
Apple MPP pre-fetches email images, artificially inflating open rates. Because open rates are now less reliable, top DTC operators rely on Revenue Per Recipient (RPR) and Click-to-Purchase conversion rates as their primary performance metrics.
