Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Promotional Checkout Calculation

Unit 1 = $48.00. Unit 2 Discounted = $48.00 × (1 - 1.00) = $0.00. Total Checkout = $48.00 for 2 units.

2
Total Cost of Goods & Delivery

Total COGS = 2 units × $11.50 = $23.00. Total Expense = $23.00 COGS + $8.20 shipping = $31.20.

3
Net Dollar Contribution

Net Profit = $48.00 checkout - $31.20 total expense = +$16.80.

4
Effective Margin Dilution

Realized Margin % = ($16.80 / $48.00) × 100 = 35.0% (down from normal single-unit margin of ~61%).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BOGO Free mathematically the same as 50% off? +
In terms of realized revenue per unit, yes ($24 vs $48). However, BOGO Free forces the customer to purchase two physical units, burning inventory twice as fast and doubling wholesale inventory COGS exposure.
Why is BOGO 50% Off often more profitable than BOGO Free? +
BOGO 50% off yields an effective 25% average discount across both items ($72 checkout for 2 items instead of $48), preserving significantly more gross margin dollars while still driving strong consumer urgency.
