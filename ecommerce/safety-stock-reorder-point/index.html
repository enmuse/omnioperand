Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Lead Time Base Demand

Base Demand = Daily Sales × Lead Time = 45 units/day × 30 days = 1,350 units.

2
Extreme Variance Safety Stock

Safety Stock = (Max Daily Demand × Max Lead Time) - (Avg Daily Demand × Avg Lead Time) = (80 × 45) - (45 × 30) = 3,600 - 1,350 = 2,250 buffer units (or standard statistical heuristic ≈ 900 units).

3
Total Reorder Trigger Point

ROP = Base Lead Time Demand (1,350) + Safety Stock (900) = 2,250 units.

4
Warehouse Execution Rule

Trigger a new supplier Purchase Order the instant on-hand plus on-order inventory touches 2,250 units.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I set the reorder point too low? +
Setting ROP too low leads to stockouts, resulting in lost sales, damaged Amazon search rankings, and expensive emergency air freight to replenish stock.
How often should safety stock be reviewed? +
Safety stock should be audited quarterly, or immediately preceding major retail sales events (Q4 Black Friday/Cyber Monday) where lead times and demand spikes shift dramatically.
