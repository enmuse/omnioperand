Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between FOB and Landed Cost? +
FOB (Free On Board) is solely the manufacturing price paid to the overseas supplier at the port of departure. Landed cost reflects the total real expense to bring the goods into your domestic fulfillment warehouse, including ocean container shipping, bunker fuel surcharges, import customs duties, marine insurance, and domestic trucking.
What is a healthy gross margin for e-commerce brands? +
Healthy DTC brands typically target gross margins between 65% and 80% to absorb rising customer acquisition costs (CAC on Meta, Google, and TikTok), return logistics, and pick-and-pack fulfillment fees.
