Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
3PL Savings = (Dropship Unit Cost × Volume) - [(Wholesale Unit Cost + 3PL Pick/Pack) × Volume + Monthly Storage]

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Monthly Dropshipping Expense

Dropship Cost = 1,800 orders × $24.50 supplier price = $44,100 per month.

2
Monthly 3PL Fulfillment Expense

3PL Variable Cost = 1,800 × ($9.20 COGS + $7.40 pick/pack) = 1,800 × $16.60 = $29,880. Total 3PL Cost = $29,880 + $450 storage = $30,330.

3
Net Monthly Savings

Monthly Savings = $44,100 - $30,330 = $13,770 saved every month by owning inventory.

4
Unit Economics & Transition Breakeven

Per-Unit Advantage = $24.50 - $16.60 = $7.90 gross saving. Breakeven Volume = $450 fixed storage / $7.90 = 57.0 ≈ 58 orders per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is it the right time to transition from dropshipping to a 3PL? +
Once a product validates consistent demand of 50 to 100 orders per month, transitioning to bulk manufacturing and a domestic 3PL cuts unit costs by 30-50% and reduces customer delivery times from 14 days to 2-3 days.
What is the primary risk of moving to a 3PL? +
Working capital risk. Bulk manufacturing requires committing upfront cash to minimum order quantities (MOQs) and carrying risk if the trend fades.
