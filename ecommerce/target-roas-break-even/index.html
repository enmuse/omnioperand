Analytical Proof & Invariants

Governing Mathematical Formula:
Break-Even ROAS = 1 / Gross Margin % | Target ROAS = 1 / (Gross Margin % - Desired Net Profit %)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Gross Contribution Margin

Contribution Margin $ = Price ($75.00) - COGS ($18.00) - Shipping ($7.50) = $49.50. Margin % = ($49.50 / $75.00) × 100 = 66.0%.

2
Break-Even ROAS Multiplier

Break-Even ROAS = 1 / Contribution Margin % = 1 / 0.660 = 1.515 ≈ 1.52×. You must generate $1.52 in revenue for every $1.00 spent on ads to avoid losing money.

3
Target ROAS with Profit Margin

Allowable Ad Spend % = Margin % (66.0%) - Desired Profit % (15.0%) = 51.0%. Target ROAS = 1 / 0.510 = 1.96×.

4
CPA Threshold Limits

Max Break-Even CPA = $49.50. Target Profitable CPA = $49.50 - ($75.00 × 15%) = $49.50 - $11.25 = $38.25.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my Meta Ads ROAS is below Break-Even ROAS? +
If your actual blended ROAS drops below break-even, you are losing cash on every customer acquired through paid ads unless repeat orders rapidly offset first-order losses.
Why does ROAS alone fail to tell the whole profit story? +
ROAS does not factor in COGS, returns, or payment fees. A 4.0× ROAS on a low-margin 20% product is actually less profitable than a 2.0× ROAS on an 85% margin digital or apparel product.
