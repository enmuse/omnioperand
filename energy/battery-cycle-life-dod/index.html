Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Cycle Life Empirical Function

Battery cycle life follows an inverse power-law with DoD. For LiFePO4: Cycles ≈ 6,000 × (0.80 / DoD)^1.3. At 80% DoD, expected cycle life = 5,000 complete cycles to 80% State of Health (SOH).

2
Daily Calendar Longevity

Years = Cycles / 365 cycles/year. 5,000 / 365 = 13.7 years.

3
Cumulative Energy Throughput

Throughput = 14.0 kWh × 0.80 DoD × 5,000 cycles = 56,000 delivered kWh.

4
Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS)

LCOS = Initial Battery Cost / Lifetime Throughput. $8,500 / 56,000 kWh = $0.151 per stored kilowatt-hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is LiFePO4 (LFP) preferred for stationary home energy storage? +
LFP features superior thermal stability (zero thermal runaway risk), non-toxic cobalt-free chemistry, and achieves 5,000 to 8,000 cycles—more than triple the cycle life of traditional NMC chemistry.
Does discharging to 100% void the battery warranty? +
Modern BMS (Battery Management Systems) enforce electronic buffers at top and bottom of charge. Discharging to '0% indicated' rarely damages modern batteries, though resting at 100% or 0% at high ambient temperatures accelerates calendar aging.
