Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
THD (%) = (√[V₂² + V₃² + V₄² + ... + Vₙ²] / V₁) × 100

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Quadrature Summation of Harmonics

V_harmonics = √(V₃² + V₅² + V₇²) = √(4.8² + 5.4² + 3.2²) = √(23.04 + 29.16 + 10.24) = √62.44 = 7.90V.

2
THD Ratio Formulation

THD = (V_harmonics / V₁) × 100 = (7.90 / 120.0) × 100 = 6.58%.

3
True RMS Waveform Magnitude

V_rms = √(V₁² + V_harmonics²) = √(120² + 7.90²) = 120.26 Volts.

4
IEEE 519 Compliance Check

IEEE 519-2022 sets a strict 5.0% maximum Voltage THD limit at the Point of Common Coupling (PCC) for systems rated ≤ 1 kV.

Frequently Asked Questions

What problems are caused by high harmonic distortion (THD)? +
Harmonic distortion causes severe overheating in transformers and neutral conductors, false tripping of circuit breakers, audible hum and vibration in motors, and premature failure of power supply capacitors.
How can high harmonics be mitigated in a facility? +
Installing active harmonic filters (AHF), 18-pulse drives, passive tuned harmonic traps, or isolation delta-wye transformers with K-factor ratings effectively cancels harmonic currents.
