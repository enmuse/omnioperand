Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Betz Limit? +
Albert Betz proved in 1919 that no wind turbine can capture more than 16/27 (59.3%) of the kinetic energy in wind. If a rotor attempted to capture 100%, the air behind it would stop completely, preventing new incoming wind from flowing through the blades.
Why does power increase with the cube of wind speed (v³)? +
Because kinetic energy is proportional to v² (½mv²), and the mass flow rate of air passing through the rotor each second is proportional to v (m = ρAv). Combining them gives power proportional to v³.
