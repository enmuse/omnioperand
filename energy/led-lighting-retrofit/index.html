Direct Wattage Reduction

3
Interactive HVAC Cooling Benefit

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HVAC interactive factor in lighting retrofits? +
Because standard incandescent, fluorescent, and HID lights generate substantial waste heat, switching to cool-running LEDs reduces building air conditioning loads by roughly 20% to 35% of the direct lighting energy savings.
What is the lifespan of commercial LEDs vs fluorescent tubes? +
High-grade commercial LED fixtures are rated for L70 lifetimes of 50,000 to 100,000 hours (over 11 years at 12 hrs/day), compared to 20,000 hours for T8 fluorescent tubes and 10,000 hours for metal halide lamps.
