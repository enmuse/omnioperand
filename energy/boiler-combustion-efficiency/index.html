Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Stack Heat Loss (%) = K · (T_stack - T_ambient) / CO₂% | Efficiency = 100% - Stack Loss - Radiation Loss

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Excess Air Quantification

Excess Air EA% = (O₂% / (20.9 - O₂%)) × 100. For 4.5% O₂: (4.5 / (20.9 - 4.5)) × 100 = 27.4% excess air.

2
Net Stack Temperature Differential

ΔT = T_flue - T_ambient = 420°F - 75°F = 345°F.

3
Siegert Combustion Stack Loss

Stack Loss % = 0.35 × (ΔT) / (21 - O₂%). With 345°F ΔT and 4.5% O₂: 0.35 × 345 / 16.5 = 7.32% dry gas loss + 9.5% latent moisture loss = 16.8% total stack loss.

4
Thermal Efficiency & Savings

Efficiency = 100% - 16.8% - 1.5% shell radiation = 81.7%. Tuning O₂ to 3.0% and installing a stack economizer yields ~4% fuel savings ($7,200+/year).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the rule of thumb for boiler stack temperature reduction? +
For every 40°F (22°C) reduction in flue gas stack temperature achieved by an economizer, overall boiler efficiency increases by approximately 1%.
What is the optimal excess oxygen level for natural gas boilers? +
Well-tuned forced-draft natural gas boilers should maintain 3.0% to 4.5% excess O₂ (15% to 25% excess air) across their firing range without producing carbon monoxide (CO).
