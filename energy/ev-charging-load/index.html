Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Continuous Charging Power Delivery

P_charge = (Volts × Amps) / 1000. At 240V and 48A: (240 × 48) / 1000 = 11.52 kW.

2
NEC 80% Continuous Load Rule

NEC Section 625 specifies that EVSE is a continuous load (runs > 3 hours). Minimum Breaker = Charging Amps / 0.80. 48A / 0.80 = 60A breaker.

3
Charge Energy Replenishment

Energy Needed ΔE = Battery Capacity × (Target% - Start%) / 100. 77.4 kWh × (80% - 20%) = 46.44 kWh.

4
Charging Duration & Recovery

Duration = (ΔE / (P_charge × 0.90 efficiency)) = 46.44 / 10.37 = 4.48 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does NEC require a 60A breaker for a 48A EV charger? +
National Electrical Code (NEC Article 625) mandates that continuous electrical loads cannot exceed 80% of the overcurrent protective device rating. 48A / 0.80 = 60A.
Can my 100A or 200A home electrical panel support Level 2 EV charging? +
A 200A panel typically accommodates a 40A or 48A charger easily. A 100A panel may require a dynamic load management device (EVEMS) to avoid expensive service upgrades.
