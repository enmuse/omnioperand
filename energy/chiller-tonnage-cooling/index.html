Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Tons of Refrigeration (TR) = (GPM × ΔT in °F) / 24 | 1 TR = 12,000 BTU/hr = 3.517 kW

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Water Temperature Differential

ΔT = Entering Water Temp - Leaving Water Temp. For 54°F EWT and 44°F LWT: ΔT = 10.0°F.

2
Hydronic Heat Transfer Rate

q = 500 × GPM × ΔT. Constant 500 = 8.33 lb/gal × 60 min/hr × 1.0 BTU/(lb·°F). For 240 GPM: 500 × 240 × 10 = 1,200,000 BTU/hr.

3
Refrigeration Tonnage Derivation

1 Ton of Refrigeration (TR) = 12,000 BTU/hr (cooling effect of melting 1 short ton of ice in 24 hours). TR = 1,200,000 / 12,000 = 100.0 TR.

4
Electrical Power Consumption

P_el = TR × (kW/Ton). 100 TR × 0.58 kW/Ton = 58.0 kW electric power demand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard rule of thumb for chilled water flow rate per ton? +
At a standard 10°F temperature differential (ΔT), chilled water systems require exactly 2.4 GPM per ton of cooling (24 / 10 = 2.4). At a 12°F ΔT, flow rate drops to 2.0 GPM/ton.
What is considered a good chiller efficiency (kW/ton)? +
High-efficiency water-cooled centrifugal and magnetic-bearing chillers achieve 0.48 to 0.58 kW/ton at full load, and down to 0.30 kW/ton at part-load IPLV.
