Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1. Inverter Power Sizing

The battery storage power inverter (BESS kW) must match the maximum delta between the facility unconstrained peak load and the target import limit: P_bess = Peak_kW - Target_kW.

2
2. Usable Energy Derating

To sustain peak shaving for duration T hours with a 90% depth-of-discharge (DoD) and 90% round-trip efficiency: Usable kWh = (P_bess × T) / (0.90 × 0.90) = (P_bess × T) / 0.81.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is peak shaving with battery storage? +
Peak shaving is the automated process of discharging an on-site battery system during periods of maximum facility power consumption to flatten spikes in grid electricity demand and reduce utility demand charges.
What is a typical demand charge on commercial utility bills? +
In the United States, commercial and industrial demand charges typically range from $12/kW to over $35/kW in states like California, New York, and Massachusetts, often comprising 30% to 60% of a company’s total electricity bill.
What C-rate is typical for peak shaving batteries? +
Peak shaving systems typically use a 0.5C (2-hour duration) to 0.25C (4-hour duration) storage architecture.
