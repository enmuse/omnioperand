Governing Mathematical Formula:
Required DC kW = Annual kWh Demand / (365 × Daily Peak Sun Hours × Derate Factor)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Daily Generation Benchmark

Target Daily Generation E_daily = (Annual kWh × Offset% / 100) / 365. For 12,000 kWh at 100%: 32.88 kWh/day.

2
DC Nameplate Power Sizing

P_dc = E_daily / (Daily Sun Hours × Derate Factor). With 4.5 sun hours and 0.82 derate factor: 32.88 / (4.5 × 0.82) = 8.91 kW DC.

3
Module Quantity Derivation

Number of Modules N = Ceil((P_dc × 1000) / Module Watts). With 410W modules: 8,910W / 410W = 21.7 ➔ 22 modules.

4
Spatial Footprint Footing

Standard 400W+ residential modules measure ~19.5 sq ft (1.82 m²). Required footprint = 22 × 19.5 ≈ 430 sq ft.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Peak Sun Hours? +
A peak sun hour is an hour during which solar irradiance averages 1,000 watts per square meter (1 kW/m²). It does not equal daylight hours.
Can I install more than 100% solar offset? +
Many utilities cap residential grid interconnection at 100% to 120% of historical 12-month electrical consumption to prevent uncompensated grid backfeeding.
