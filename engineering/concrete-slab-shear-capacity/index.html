Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Critical Perimeter at d/2 Face

ACI 318-19 Section 22.6 specifies punching shear failure occurs on a truncated pyramid perimeter at distance d/2 from the column face. b₀ = 4 × (Column Width + d) = 4 × (18" + 7") = 4 × 25" = 100 inches.

2
Nominal Concrete Shear Stress

v_c = 4 · λ · √(f'c) = 4 · 1.0 · √4000 = 4 × 63.245 = 252.98 PSI.

3
Nominal Punching Shear Strength (V_c)

V_c = v_c · b₀ · d = 252.98 PSI × 100" × 7.0" = 177,086 lbs = 177.09 kips.

4
Design Shear Strength (φV_c)

Strength reduction factor φ = 0.75 for shear. φV_c = 0.75 × 177.09 = 132.82 kips allowable column reaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is punching shear the most dangerous failure mode in flat-slab buildings? +
Punching shear is a sudden, brittle failure with zero warning signs. The floor slab punches downward around the column head, which can trigger a progressive 'pancake' collapse of entire multi-story concrete structures.
How do structural engineers increase punching shear capacity? +
Increase slab thickness, enlarge column capitals, add drop panels, or cast vertical shear reinforcement studs (stud rails) within the slab around column perimeters.
