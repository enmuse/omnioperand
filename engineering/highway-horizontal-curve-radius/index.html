Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Centrifugal Force Equilibrium

Centrifugal force pushing a car outward must be counterbalanced by pavement superelevation slope (e) and tire side friction (f): e + f = V² / (15 · R).

2
AASHTO Minimum Radius Derivation

R_min = V² / [15 · (0.01 · e + f)]. For V = 65 mph, e = 6.0%, and f = 0.11: R_min = 65² / [15 · (0.06 + 0.11)] = 4,225 / [15 · 0.17] = 4,225 / 2.55 = 1,657 feet.

3
Degree of Curvature Formulation

Degree of Curve D = 5,729.58 / R = 5,729.58 / 1,657 = 3.46°.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Superelevation in highway engineering? +
Superelevation is the lateral banking of a roadway curve, raising the outer edge of the pavement to use gravity to help vehicles turn safely without skidding off the road.
Why is maximum superelevation capped at 6% or 8% in freezing climates? +
If superelevation is too steep (e.g. 10% or 12%), slow-moving or stopped trucks on an icy highway will slide sideways down the banked slope into the inside ditch.
