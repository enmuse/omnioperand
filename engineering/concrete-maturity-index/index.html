Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Nurse-Saul Temperature-Time Formulation

ASTM C1074 sets standard datum temperature T₀ = -10°C (temperature below which hydration stops). Effective Thermal Increment = T - T₀ = 28°C - (-10°C) = 38°C.

2
Cumulative Maturity Calculation

Maturity Index = 38°C × 72 hours = 2,736 °C-hours.

3
Logarithmic Strength Correlation

Using calibrated hyperbolic curve S = S_inf · [M / (k + M)]: At 2,736 °C-hours, in-place compressive strength reaches 3,480 PSI, satisfying the 3,000 PSI requirement to strip formwork 2 days ahead of schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ASTM C1074 Maturity Method in concrete construction? +
ASTM C1074 is a non-destructive testing method that uses embedded temperature sensors to continuously log concrete hydration heat, correlating real-time maturity index to laboratory calibration curves to determine exact in-place compressive strength.
How does concrete maturity save money for commercial contractors? +
Instead of waiting 7 to 14 days for traditional lab cylinder breaks, contractors verify in-place strength in 48 to 72 hours, allowing them to strip forms and post-tension slabs days earlier, accelerating high-rise construction cycles.
