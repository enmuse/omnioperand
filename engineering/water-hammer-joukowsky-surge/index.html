Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Acoustic Wave Speed in Elastic Steel

Water bulk modulus K = 319,000 psi. In standard steel pipe, elasticity reduces wave speed from 4,860 fps (pure water) down to c = 3,820 ft/second.

2
Joukowsky Momentum Equation

ΔP = (ρ · c · Δv) / 144g. Density of water ρ = 62.4 lb/cu ft. g = 32.174 ft/s².

3
Pressure Spike Evaluation

ΔP = (62.4 × 3,820 × 8.0) / (144 × 32.174) = 1,906,944 / 4,633 = 411.6 PSI pressure spike above baseline line pressure.

4
Failure Prevention

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the critical valve closure time (2L/c)? +
If a valve closes faster than the time it takes the pressure wave to travel to the reservoir and reflect back (T_crit = 2L/c), maximum Joukowsky water hammer pressure develops. If closure time is significantly longer than 2L/c, the surge is substantially attenuated.
Why do PVC pipes experience lower water hammer spikes than steel? +
PVC is much more flexible (lower elastic modulus E) than steel. The flexible plastic pipe wall expands elastically to absorb the kinetic shockwave, reducing wave speed to ~1,200 fps and cutting the pressure spike to one-third of steel.
