Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Inlet Control and Outlet Control? +
Inlet Control occurs when the culvert barrel can carry more water than the inlet opening can admit, so flow capacity is governed strictly by inlet geometry. Outlet Control occurs when tailwater, pipe friction, and barrel slope choke the flow, causing water to back up along the entire pipe length.
What is the allowable HW/D ratio for highway culvert design? +
State DOT standards generally limit Headwater-to-Diameter (HW/D) to 1.2 for secondary roads and 1.5 for primary highways to maintain minimum 2 feet of freeboard below the edge of the travel lane.
