Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Detention Basin and a Retention Basin? +
A Detention Basin (dry pond) temporarily holds peak stormwater and drains completely dry between storms within 24 to 48 hours. A Retention Basin (wet pond) maintains a permanent pool of water year-round for biological pollutant removal.
Why do municipalities require pre-development runoff matching? +
Urbanization replaces permeable soil with impervious asphalt and rooftops, dramatically increasing runoff volume. Mandating that post-construction discharge match pre-development rates prevents downstream river flooding and streambank erosion.
