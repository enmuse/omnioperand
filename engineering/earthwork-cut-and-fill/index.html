Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Bank, Loose, and Compacted cubic yards? +
Bank (In-situ): Soil in its natural undisturbed ground state. Loose: Soil after excavation that has expanded (swelled by 15-25%). Compacted: Soil rolled with heavy machinery into a dense fill embankment (shrinks by 10-15% below bank volume).
When is the Prismoidal formula preferred over Average End Area? +
Average End Area tends to slightly overestimate volumes (typically by 1% to 3%) when adjacent cross-section areas differ significantly. The Prismoidal formula incorporates a middle cross-section for higher precision in steep, irregular terrain.
