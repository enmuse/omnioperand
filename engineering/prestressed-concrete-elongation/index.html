Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Strand Cross-Sectional Area

One 0.5-inch 270k strand = 0.153 sq inches. For 4 strands: A_ps = 4 × 0.153 = 0.612 sq inches.

2
Average Effective Force Accounting for Friction

Due to angular curvature and wobble friction, jacking force decays along the tendon: P_avg ≈ Jacking Force × 0.947 = 133.2 × 0.947 = 126.14 kips.

3
Hooke's Law Elastic Elongation

ΔL = (P_avg · L) / (A_ps · E_ps). L = 120 ft × 12 = 1,440 inches. E_ps = 28,500 ksi. ΔL = (126.14 × 1,440) / (0.612 × 28,500) = 181,641 / 17,442 = 10.41 inches.

4
ACI Field Verification Mandate

ACI 318 Section 26.10 mandates measured field jack elongation must match theoretical calculations within ±7% (9.68" to 11.14") to verify lack of tendon binding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why must post-tension tendon elongation be measured on site? +
Measuring how many inches the hydraulic jack stretches the steel tendon provides direct physical proof that the engineered prestress force has traveled all the way through the concrete slab without pinching, kinking, or binding.
What happens if measured elongation is outside ±7% tolerance? +
If elongation is too short, the tendon bound up in the duct, leaving the concrete under-stressed (cracking hazard). If too long, the tendon may have slipped in the dead-end anchor or steel strands broke.
