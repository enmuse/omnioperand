Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Response Modification Factor R in seismic design? +
Factor R represents a structural system's inherent ability to dissipate earthquake energy through ductile inelastic yielding (bending without snapping). A ductile steel moment frame has R=8.0, reducing required design forces by a factor of 8 compared to an elastic brittle structure.
What is included in the effective seismic weight (W)? +
Total building structural dead weight, plus permanent equipment, 25% of storage floor live load, total partition allowance (minimum 10 PSF), and 20% of design snow load if roof snow exceeds 30 PSF.
