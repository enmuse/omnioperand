Unit Dimensional Parity

1 acre = 43,560 sq ft. 1 inch/hour = 1/12 ft / 3,600s = 1/43,200 ft/s. 43,560 / 43,200 = 1.0083 CFS per acre-inch/hr. Therefore, Q ≈ C · I · A directly in CFS.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum watershed size for the Rational Method? +
The Rational Method is strictly valid only for small catchments under 200 acres (or under 1 square mile). For larger watersheds, hydrologists must use unit hydrograph methods (NRCS TR-55 or HEC-HMS) to account for spatial rainfall variation and routing delay.
What is the Time of Concentration (Tc)? +
Time of concentration is the time required for water to travel from the most hydraulically remote point in the watershed to the outlet. In the Rational Method, the rainfall intensity (I) used MUST equal the storm duration corresponding to Tc.
