Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
q_ult = (c · Nc · sc) + (q · Nq) + (0.5 · γ · B · Nγ · sγ) | q_all = q_ult / Safety Factor (typically 3.0)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Terzaghi Bearing Capacity Factors (ϕ = 28°)

Nc = 31.61, Nq = 17.81, Nγ = 15.70. Shape factors for square footing: sc = 1.3, sγ = 0.8.

2
Overburden Surcharge at Base

q = γ · Df = 115 PCF × 4.0 ft = 460.0 PSF.

3
Terzaghi General Bearing Equation

q_ult = (1.3 · c · Nc) + (q · Nq) + (0.4 · γ · B · Nγ) = (1.3 × 250 × 31.61) + (460 × 17.81) + (0.4 × 115 × 6 × 15.70) = 10,273 + 8,193 + 4,333 = 22,799 PSF.

4
Engineering Safety Factor Application

q_all = q_ult / FS = 22,799 / 3.0 = 7,600 PSF. Maximum column load on 6x6 footing = 7,600 × 36 sq ft = 273,600 lbs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a Factor of Safety of 3.0 standard for foundation bearing capacity? +
A high safety factor of 3.0 is required in geotechnical engineering due to inherent soil variability across a building site, sampling disturbances, and to strictly limit total settlement to under 1.0 inch.
What happens if the groundwater table rises above footing depth? +
When groundwater submerges the footing, the soil's effective unit weight drops by nearly 50% (buoyant unit weight γ' = γ_sat - 62.4 PCF), cutting the soil self-weight bearing component in half.
