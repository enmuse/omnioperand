Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Standard and Modified Proctor tests? +
Standard Proctor (ASTM D698) uses a 5.5-lb hammer dropped from 12 inches (12,375 ft-lb/cu ft compactive energy). Modified Proctor (ASTM D1557) uses a 10-lb hammer dropped from 18 inches (56,250 ft-lb/cu ft), simulating heavy airfield and highway axle loads.
Why does adding water increase soil compaction up to an optimum point? +
Water acts as a lubricant between soil grains, allowing particles to slide into a tight, dense interlocking packing structure until the Optimum Moisture Content (OMC) is reached. Beyond OMC, water fills pore spaces and displaces solid soil grains, reducing density.
