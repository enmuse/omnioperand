Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between ASCE 7 Exposure B, C, and D? +
Exposure B applies to urban, suburban, and wooded areas with closely spaced obstructions. Exposure C applies to open flat grasslands and agricultural plains. Exposure D applies to unobstructed flat coastal water surfaces extending at least 1 mile inland.
Why are wind suction pressures on roof corners so much higher than wall pressures? +
When wind hits a sharp building edge, intense localized flow separation vortices peel over the roof perimeter, creating localized negative suction uplift forces 2 to 3 times higher than wall stagnation pressures.
