Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if bridge expansion joints jam or freeze with debris? +
When summer heat causes bridge girders to expand against a jammed joint, immense thermal stresses develop (over 10,000 PSI), crushing abutment backwalls, blowing out bridge bearings, and spalling concrete decks.
Why is the setting gap adjusted based on installation temperature? +
The joint cannot simply be installed at half-width. If installed on a cold 30°F winter day, the gap must be left wide open because the bridge will expand significantly come summer. If installed on a 90°F day, the gap must be set very narrow.
