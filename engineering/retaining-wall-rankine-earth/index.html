Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Rankine Active Earth Coefficient (Ka)

Ka = tan²(45° - ϕ / 2). For ϕ = 32°: Ka = tan²(45° - 16°) = tan²(29°) = 0.5543² = 0.3073.

2
Triangular Pressure Distribution

Lateral pressure increases linearly with depth: Pressure at base σ_h = Ka · γ · H = 0.3073 × 120 PCF × 12 ft = 442.5 lbs/sq ft.

3
Total Resultant Force (P_a)

P_a = ½ · σ_h · H = ½ × 442.5 × 12 = 2,655.0 lbs per linear foot of wall, acting at H/3 (4.0 ft above the base).

4
Overturning Moment About Toe

Moment M_ot = P_a × (H / 3) = 2,655.0 × 4.0 ft = 10,620.0 ft-lbs per linear foot.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Active (Ka), At-Rest (K0), and Passive (Kp) earth pressure? +
Active pressure (Ka ~0.3) develops when the wall tilts slightly away from the soil, mobilizing internal friction. At-Rest (K0 ~0.5) applies to rigid basement walls that cannot deflect. Passive pressure (Kp ~3.0+) develops when the wall is pushed violently INTO the soil, generating massive resisting force.
Why is free-draining gravel backfill required behind retaining walls? +
If water pools behind a retaining wall without drainage weep holes, hydrostatic water pressure adds 62.4 lbs/sq ft per foot of depth, tripling total overturning forces and causing sudden catastrophic wall blowouts.
