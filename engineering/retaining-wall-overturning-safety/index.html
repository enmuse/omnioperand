Governing Mathematical Formula:
Ka = tan²(45° - ϕ/2) | Pa = ½ · Ka · γ · H² | M_ot = Pa · (H/3) | FS_ot = M_res / M_ot ≥ 2.0

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Rankine Active Earth Pressure

According to Rankine earth pressure theory, lateral active pressure coefficient is Ka = tan²(45° - ϕ/2) = (1 - sin ϕ) / (1 + sin ϕ). Total horizontal force is Pa = ½ · Ka · γ · H² acting at H/3 above base.

2
2. Overturning Moment Formulation

The overturning moment about the toe of the footing is M_ot = Pa × (H/3).

3
3. Resisting Equilibrium & Stability Ratios

Resisting moment M_res is produced by the weight of concrete (stem and footing) and the heavy wedge of soil resting above the heel. Factor of safety against overturning is FS_ot = M_res / M_ot (minimum 2.0 required by IBC). Factor of safety against sliding is FS_slide = (μ · Σ Vertical) / Pa (minimum 1.5 required).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a safe factor of safety against overturning for retaining walls? +
The International Building Code (IBC Section 1807.2) requires a minimum Factor of Safety of 2.0 against overturning, and a minimum Factor of Safety of 1.5 against sliding.
Why is the soil above the heel beneficial for retaining walls? +
The heel extends backward into the backfill, using the massive vertical weight of the retained soil column to anchor the wall against overturning.
What happens if a retaining wall fails in sliding? +
If the lateral earth thrust exceeds the frictional resistance between the concrete footing and foundation subsoil (FS < 1.5), the entire wall will shift laterally, requiring a shear key.
