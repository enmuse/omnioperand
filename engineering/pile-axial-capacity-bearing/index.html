Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Friction Piles and End-Bearing Piles? +
Friction piles derive the vast majority of their load capacity from shearing resistance along the embedded sides of the pile shaft in clay/sand. End-bearing piles pass through soft upper soils to rest their tip directly on unyielding dense bedrock or cemented hardpan.
What is pile setup (freeze)? +
During driving, soil pore water pressure spikes, temporarily reducing resistance. Over the next 24 to 72 hours, pore pressures dissipate and soil reconsolidates against the pile, significantly increasing load capacity (known as 'set-up' or freeze).
