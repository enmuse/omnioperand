Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Infinite Slope Simplified Formulation

FS = [c' / (γ · z · sin β · cos β)] + [tan ϕ' / tan β] · (1 - r_u · sec² β).

2
Resisting vs Driving Equilibrium

Driving shear stress = gravity component parallel to failure plane (sin β). Resisting shear strength = cohesion c' + effective normal stress (σ - u) · tan ϕ'.

3
Pore Water Pressure Effect

Rising groundwater buoyancy reduces intergranular contact stress between soil particles, drastically reducing frictional shear resistance by 22.5%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum required Factor of Safety for engineered slopes? +
Standard civil engineering practice requires a minimum Factor of Safety of 1.50 for permanent slopes supporting structures, 1.30 for temporary construction cuts, and 1.10 under extreme seismic earthquake loading.
Why do landslides usually occur during heavy rainfall? +
Rainwater saturates pore spaces between soil grains, generating high positive pore water pressure (u) that acts like a hydraulic jack, lifting soil particles apart and eliminating frictional shear strength.
