Governing Mathematical Formula:
Flow Q = (1.486 / n) · Area · (Hydraulic Radius)^(2/3) · (Slope)^(1/2)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Geometric Wetted Area and Perimeter

For a trapezoidal channel with 2:1 side slopes (z = 2): Area A = (b + zy) · y = (6 + 2×2.5) × 2.5 = 11.0 × 2.5 = 27.5 sq ft. Wetted Perimeter P = b + 2y√(1 + z²) = 6 + 2(2.5)√5 = 6 + 11.18 = 17.18 ft.

2
Hydraulic Radius R

R = Area / Wetted Perimeter = 27.5 / 17.18 = 1.601 ft.

3
Manning's Empirical Velocity Equation

v = (1.486 / n) · R^(2/3) · S^(1/2) = (1.486 / 0.035) × 1.601^(0.667) × 0.005^(0.5) = 42.457 × 1.368 × 0.07071 = 4.11 ft/sec.

4
Total Discharge Capacity

Q = v · A = 4.11 ft/s × 27.5 sq ft = 113.0 CFS (cubic feet per second).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Froude Number in open channel hydraulics? +
The Froude number (Fr = v / √(g · D)) represents the ratio of inertial forces to gravity forces. Fr < 1 is tranquil subcritical flow; Fr = 1 is critical flow; Fr > 1 is rapid supercritical flow prone to hydraulic jumps.
What is an acceptable velocity in grass-lined drainage swales? +
To prevent soil scour and sod tearing, grassed waterways are typically limited to velocities between 3.0 and 5.0 fps depending on grass species and soil cohesiveness.
