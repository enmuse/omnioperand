Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I exceed my monthly request quota? +
When your monthly quota is reached, subsequent requests return an informative HTTP 429 response detailing when your quota resets. We do not charge surprise overages; you can seamlessly upgrade your tier in the Stripe customer billing portal at any time.
How does white-label widget embedding work? +
Subscribers on Pro and Enterprise tiers receive a verified Partner ID. Adding data-partner-id="YOUR_PRO_ID" to your embed snippet automatically removes the "Powered by OmniOperand" badge and allows you to inject custom CSS brand colors.
Can I connect the MCP Server to Claude Desktop or Cursor? +
Yes! Our Model Context Protocol server runs via stdio or SSE. Simply add node mcp/server.js to your claude_desktop_config.json or Cursor MCP settings to empower your AI assistant with deterministic math ground truth.
