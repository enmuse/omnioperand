Formula Matematica Fondamentale:
P(A|B) = [P(B|A) · P(A)] / P(B)

Dimostrazione Matematica Passo dopo Passo

1
Conditional Probability Definition

Joint probability P(A ∩ B) = P(A|B) · P(B) = P(B|A) · P(A). Equating both sides yields Bayes' Theorem: P(A|B) = [P(B|A) · P(A)] / P(B).

2
Law of Total Probability

The marginal evidence probability partitions across mutually exclusive hypotheses: P(B) = P(B|A) · P(A) + P(B|¬A) · P(¬A), where P(¬A) = 1 - P(A).

3
Base Rate Fallacy Resolution

When prior probability P(A) is very low (e.g. rare disease or rare defect), even a test with 95% sensitivity produces predominantly false positive signals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Base Rate Fallacy? +
The base rate fallacy is the cognitive error of judging conditional probability without factoring in background prior probability. If a rare disease affects 1 in 1,000 people, a 95% accurate test will still be wrong over 95% of the time on a positive result.
How is Bayes' theorem used in machine learning and AI? +
Bayesian inference powers spam filters, medical diagnostic AI, Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) simulations, and Kalman filters in autonomous vehicle navigation by continuously updating probabilities as new evidence arrives.
