Demonstração Matemática Passo a Passo

1
Orthodiagonal Quadrilateral Area Theorem

Because the diagonals of a kite intersect at right angles (90°), its area is identical to half the bounding rectangle: Area = ½(d₁ · d₂).

2
Pythagorean Pair Formulation

Let d₂ be bisected into two equal halves (d₂/2). Top sides: a = √[(d₁_top)² + (d₂/2)²]. Bottom sides: b = √[(d₁ - d₁_top)² + (d₂/2)²].

3
Perimeter Sum

Perimeter P = 2(a + b).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the geometric definition of a kite? +
A kite is a quadrilateral with reflection symmetry across a diagonal, having two distinct pairs of adjacent, equal-length sides.
Can a kite be a cyclic quadrilateral? +
A kite is cyclic (can be inscribed in a circle) if and only if its two opposite non-vertex angles are right angles (90°), in which case it is called a right kite.
