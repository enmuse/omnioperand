Frequently Asked Questions

What is the consequence of incorrect sprayer speed? +
Sprayer output (GPA) is inversely proportional to speed. Driving 20% faster than calibrated under-applies chemical by 20% (causing weed control failure). Driving 20% slower over-applies chemical, risking illegal crop stunting and chemical carryover.
What is the standard order for mixing multiple chemicals in a tank (WALES rule)? +
1) Water conditioning agents / AMS; 2) Wettable powders & dry granules; 3) Agitate thoroughly; 4) Liquid flowables; 5) Emulsifiable concentrates; 6) Surfactants / crop oil concentrates.
