Frequently Asked Questions

How many pounds of grapes are in one bottle of wine? +
On average, one standard 750ml bottle of wine requires approximately 2.6 to 2.8 pounds (1.2 kg) of fresh harvested grapes, or roughly 600 to 800 individual grape berries.
Why do high-end wineries intentionally reduce crop yield per acre? +
Limiting yields (e.g. dropping green fruit clusters to maintain 2.5 tons/acre) forces the vine to concentrate sugars, polyphenols, tannins, and anthocyanin pigments into fewer grapes, creating dramatically superior wine complexity and ageability.
