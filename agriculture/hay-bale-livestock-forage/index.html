Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Cylindrical Bale Volume

Bale Volume = π · r² · w = π · 2.5² · 4 = 78.54 cubic feet.

2
Density Mass Conversion

Average dry hay density = 11.25 lbs/cu ft. Bale Weight = 78.54 × 11.25 = 883.6 lbs.

3
Herd Winter Daily Requirement

A 1,200-lb beef cow consumes ~30 lbs hay/day (2.5% body weight). 45 cows × 30 lbs = 1,350 lbs/day.

4

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a 5x5 bale weigh almost double a 4x4 bale? +
Because volume scales with the SQUARE of the diameter (r²). A 5x5 bale contains nearly 65% more volume and hay mass than a 4x4 bale, making small differences in bale diameter deceptive.
How can ranchers reduce round bale feeding waste? +
Using cone round bale feeders with solid bottom sheet-metal skirts cuts hay waste from 25% down to under 5% compared to unrolling hay on muddy ground.
