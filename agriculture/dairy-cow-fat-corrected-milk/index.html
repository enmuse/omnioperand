Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fat-Corrected Milk (FCM)? +
FCM standardizes milk yields to an equivalent energy basis of 4.0% fat, allowing dairy researchers and nutritionists to accurately assess feed conversion efficiency regardless of component differences.
Why are dairy farmers paid based on components rather than liquid volume? +
Under Federal Milk Marketing Orders (FMMO), modern processors pay premiums for butterfat and protein solids used to manufacture cheese and butter, rather than the water volume.
