Frequently Asked Questions

What is 'Gear Up and Throttle Down' (GUTD)? +
GUTD is a fuel-saving technique for light to moderate tractor loads where the operator shifts into a higher gear and throttles back engine RPM to maintain the same ground speed, saving 15% to 25% on diesel fuel.
Why is off-road dyed diesel cheaper than highway diesel? +
Dyed off-road diesel is exempt from federal and state highway road excise taxes (~$0.40 to $0.65/gallon) because farm tractors and combines operate on private farmland rather than public paved roads.
