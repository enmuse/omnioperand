Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 5,940 constant in sprayer calibration? +
5,940 is the mathematical conversion constant derived from: (43,560 sq ft/acre × 12 inches/ft) / (5,280 ft/mile × 60 minutes/hour).
Why is high water carrier volume (20+ GPA) needed for pasture weeds? +
Unlike row crops where small weeds are exposed, mature pasture weeds (thistles, blackberry briars, multiflora rose) have dense thickets requiring 20 to 25 GPA to penetrate the canopy and achieve thorough leaf contact.
