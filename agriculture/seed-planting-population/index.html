Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Linear Feet per Acre

One acre = 43,560 sq ft. In 30-inch rows (2.5 ft): Linear Feet/Acre = 43,560 / 2.5 = 17,424 linear feet of row per acre.

2
Seed Density per Foot

Seeds per Foot = 34,000 seeds / 17,424 feet = 1.951 seeds per foot.

3
Seed In-Row Spacing

Seed Spacing = 12 inches / 1.951 = 6.15 inches apart in the trench.

4
Commercial Seed Bag Units

Total Seeds = 34,000 × 180 acres = 6,120,000 seeds. Divided by standard 80,000-kernel corn bag = 76.5 bags (order 77 bags).

Frequently Asked Questions

How does germination rate affect seeding population? +
Seed tags list certified germination (typically 95%). To achieve a target harvestable stand of 32,000 plants per acre, you must plant at 34,000 seeds/acre to account for germination failures and seedling mortality.
What is the trend toward narrow 20-inch corn rows? +
20-inch rows space plants more equidistant from each other, allowing the leaf canopy to close faster (shading weeds and reducing evaporation), often yielding 3% to 5% more bushels on high-yield soils.
