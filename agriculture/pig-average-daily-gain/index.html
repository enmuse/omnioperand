Frequently Asked Questions

What is an industry standard ADG for market hogs? +
Modern lean-genetics market pigs achieve Average Daily Gains of 1.85 to 2.15 pounds per day during the grow-finish phase, reaching 280 pounds in approximately 110 to 125 days after leaving the nursery.
How does barn temperature impact swine ADG and FCR? +
Hogs outside their thermal-neutral zone (65°F to 72°F) redirect dietary calories away from muscle accretion toward shivering to stay warm or panting to stay cool, deteriorating FCR by 10% to 15%.
