Analytical Proof & Invariants

Governing Mathematical Formula:
AUM = (Total Forage lbs/acre × Harvest Efficiency 25%) / (800 lbs dry matter per AUM)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Total Pasture Forage Production

Total Forage = 160 acres × 2,400 lbs/acre = 384,000 lbs of standing dry matter.

2
Take-Half / Leave-Half Rule

To preserve root vitality and prevent soil erosion, grazing efficiency is capped at 25% (50% consumed, half of which is trampled/lost). Usable Forage = 384,000 × 25% = 96,000 lbs.

3
Animal Unit Equivalent (AUE)

Standard 1.0 AU is defined as a 1,000-lb cow consuming ~780-800 lbs dry matter per month (2.6% of body weight daily). A 1,250-lb cow = 1.25 AU, consuming 975 lbs/month.

4
Herd Capacity Resolution

Forage per Cow for 6 months = 975 lbs × 6 = 5,850 lbs. Herd Capacity = 96,000 lbs / 5,850 lbs = 16.4 cows (or ~21 cows for smaller 1,100-lb cattle).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Animal Unit Month (AUM)? +
An Animal Unit Month (AUM) is the amount of dry forage required to sustain one standard 1,000-pound cow and her nursing calf for one month, standardized at approximately 780 to 800 pounds of dry matter.
Why does the 'Take Half, Leave Half' rule matter? +
Leaving 50% of plant biomass allows pasture grasses to maintain deep root systems, store carbohydrate reserves for winter survival, shade soil to conserve moisture, and prevent noxious weed invasion.
