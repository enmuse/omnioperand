Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
DLI Deficit Formulation

DLI Deficit = Target DLI (17.0) - Natural Glazing DLI (8.5) = 8.50 mol/m²/day needed from grow lights.

2
Micromole-Second Conversion

8.50 mol = 8,500,000 μmol. Total seconds in 16 hours = 16 × 3,600 = 57,600 seconds.

3
Required Continuous PPFD Flux

PPFD = 8,500,000 / 57,600 = 147.57 μmol/m²/second of photosynthetic light across the crop canopy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Daily Light Integral (DLI)? +
DLI measures the total cumulative number of photosynthetic photons (moles of light in the PAR 400-700nm waveband) delivered to a one-square-meter area over a 24-hour day, functioning like a 'rain gauge for light.'
How does greenhouse glazing reduce natural solar DLI? +
Standard double-poly or glass greenhouse cladding reduces incoming outdoor solar radiation by 30% to 40% through reflection, structural shading, and dirt accumulation.
