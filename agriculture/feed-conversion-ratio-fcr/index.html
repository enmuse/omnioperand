Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good Feed Conversion Ratio for different livestock species? +
Aquaculture salmon is most efficient (~1.1 to 1.3:1); Broiler chickens (~1.6 to 1.8:1); Market hogs (~2.7 to 3.0:1); Feedlot beef cattle (~6.0 to 7.0:1 on dry matter basis).
How does improving FCR by 0.5 impact feedlot profitability? +
Improving beef FCR from 6.5 to 6.0 saves 350 lbs of feed per steer (700 lbs gain × 0.5). At $285/ton ($0.1425/lb), this puts an extra $49.88 profit in the producer's pocket per animal.
