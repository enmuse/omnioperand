Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Phosphate & DAP Balancing

Diammonium Phosphate (18-46-0) contains 46% P₂O₅. DAP Rate = 70 lbs P₂O₅ / 0.46 = 152.2 lbs DAP/acre. 152.2 lbs DAP also delivers 152.2 × 18% = 27.4 lbs of free Nitrogen.

2
Residual Nitrogen Balance (Urea)

Remaining N needed = 180 - 27.4 = 152.6 lbs N. Urea (46-0-0) rate = 152.6 / 0.46 = 331.7 lbs Urea/acre.

3
Potash Application (0-0-60)

Muriate of Potash (0-0-60) contains 60% K₂O. Potash Rate = 90 / 0.60 = 150.0 lbs/acre.

4
Field Scale Tender Volume

Total product per acre = 152.2 + 331.7 + 150 = 633.9 lbs/acre. For 120 acres: (633.9 × 120) / 2,000 lbs = 38.03 tons.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the three numbers on a fertilizer bag mean? +
The three numbers represent the guaranteed minimum percentage by weight of Nitrogen (N), available Phosphate (P₂O₅), and soluble Potash (K₂O). For example, 100 lbs of 46-0-0 urea contains exactly 46 lbs of pure Nitrogen.
Why must Nitrogen credit from DAP or MAP be subtracted from Urea? +
Because starter phosphates like DAP (18-46-0) and MAP (11-52-0) contain substantial nitrogen, failing to credit it results in over-application of urea, wasting money and causing environmental nitrate runoff.
