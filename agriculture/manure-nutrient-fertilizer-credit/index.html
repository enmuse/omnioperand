Frequently Asked Questions

Why is only 30% to 50% of manure Nitrogen available in the first year? +
Much of the nitrogen in solid manure is bound in complex organic matter and requires microbial mineralization over several seasons before becoming plant-available nitrate/ammonium.
How does incorporation timing affect manure nitrogen loss? +
Surface-applied liquid manure loses up to 50% of its ammonium nitrogen through ammonia volatilization into the atmosphere within 24 to 48 hours unless incorporated with a disk or injected directly into the soil.
