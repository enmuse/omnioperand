Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Demurrage and Detention? +
Demurrage is charged by shipping lines and marine terminals while a loaded container remains inside the port terminal past free time. Detention (per diem) is charged when the container has been pulled from the port but is held at an off-site warehouse and not returned to the empty depot on time.
What is the FMC Ocean Shipping Reform Act (OSRA)? +
OSRA 2022 established strict Federal Maritime Commission rules prohibiting ocean carriers from assessing demurrage and detention charges when port congestion or chassis shortages make container pickup physically impossible.
