Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Arrhenius Temperature Weighting

Mean Kinetic Temperature accounts for the exponential increase in chemical degradation rates at higher temperatures, unlike simple arithmetic averages.

2
Kelvin Conversion & Exponentiation

T₁ = 5°C = 278.15 K (42 hrs). T₂ = 14.5°C = 287.65 K (6 hrs). Activation Energy ΔH/R = 10,000 K (standard USP constant).

3
Logarithmic Summation

Weighted Sum = (42 × e^(-10000/278.15) + 6 × e^(-10000/287.65)) / 48 = e^(-35.72).

4
MKT Final Resolution

MKT = 10,000 / 35.72 = 279.95 K = 6.80°C. Because MKT ≤ 8.0°C and transient spike remained below 15°C, the vaccine batch remains potent.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mean Kinetic Temperature (MKT)? +
MKT is a single calculated temperature that expresses the equivalent thermal stress experienced by a pharmaceutical product over time, reflecting how chemical reaction rates accelerate at higher temperatures based on the Arrhenius equation.
Can MKT be used to justify freezing excursions? +
No! MKT applies strictly to heat excursions. Freezing excursions can cause irreversible physical denaturation of protein biologics and emulsion breakage, which MKT math does not capture.
