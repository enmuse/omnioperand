Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum distance where intermodal rail makes financial sense? +
Domestic intermodal rail generally requires a minimum lane length of 600 to 750 miles. On shorter lanes, the double drayage costs at both ends negate the rail linehaul cost advantage.
Is intermodal shipping slower than highway trucking? +
Yes. Intermodal typically adds 1 to 2 days of transit time compared to solo OTR trucking due to train assembly at classification yards and ramp drayage appointment scheduling.
