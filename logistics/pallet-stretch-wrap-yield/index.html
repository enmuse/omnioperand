Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between pre-stretch and tension stretch? +
Pre-stretch is the mechanical elongation of film between two geared rollers inside the carriage head before it leaves the machine. Tension stretch is the pulling force applied between the carriage and the turning pallet.
What is proper containment force for freight shipping? +
Light stable loads (tissue paper) require 2 to 5 lbs of containment force; standard freight requires 7 to 12 lbs; heavy or unstable loads (beverages, drums) require 15 to 22 lbs to prevent transit failure.
