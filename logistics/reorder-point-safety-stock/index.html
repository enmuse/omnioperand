Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
ROP = (Daily Demand × Lead Time) + (Z × √[Lead Time × σ_demand² + Demand² × σ_leadtime²])

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Base Lead Time Demand

Expected Lead Time Demand D_L = Daily Demand × Lead Time = 85 × 21 = 1,785 units.

2
Standard Deviation over Lead Time

σ_LT = √(Lead Time) × σ_demand = √21 × 18 = 4.5826 × 18 = 82.49 units.

3
Z-Factor Service Level Multiplier

A 95% cycle service level corresponds to a standard normal Z-score of 1.645.

4
Safety Stock & Reorder Point

Safety Stock SS = Z × σ_LT = 1.645 × 82.49 = 135.7 ≈ 136 units. Reorder Point ROP = 1,785 + 136 = 1,921 units. Place PO the moment stock touches 1,921.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I set service level to 99% vs 95%? +
Moving from 95% (Z=1.65) to 99% (Z=2.33) requires increasing your safety stock buffer by over 41%, tying up substantially more working capital in warehouse inventory to protect against rare demand surges.
What is the most effective way to lower the Reorder Point? +
Shortening supplier lead times (e.g. nearshoring from 60 days to 14 days) drastically shrinks both lead time demand and the lead time standard deviation √L, slashing required safety stock.
