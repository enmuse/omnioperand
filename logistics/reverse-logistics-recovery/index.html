Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average return rate in e-commerce vs brick-and-mortar? +
Online apparel and e-commerce return rates average 20% to 30% (and up to 40% during peak holiday season), compared to 8% to 10% for physical brick-and-mortar stores.
What is a 'Keep It' return refund policy? +
For bulky, low-margin, or hazardous products where return freight ($12+) exceeds the item's salvage value, retailers use algorithmic rules to instantly issue a full refund while instructing the customer to keep or donate the item.
