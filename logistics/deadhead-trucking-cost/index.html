Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a healthy deadhead percentage in freight? +
Industry benchmark fleets maintain deadhead rates below 10% to 15%. Over 20% deadhead severely erodes carrier profitability unless offset by high headhaul backhaul rates.
Why is deadhead cost nearly identical to loaded cost? +
Although an empty truck gets slightly better fuel economy (7.5 MPG empty vs 6.5 MPG loaded), fixed truck depreciation, driver wages, insurance, and tolls remain identical whether cargo is loaded or not.
