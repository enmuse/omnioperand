Frequently Asked Questions

What is the primary benefit of cross-docking vs traditional warehousing? +
Cross-docking eliminates put-away and storage costs, reduces inventory holding costs, slashes order cycle times from days to hours, and minimizes product damage from repeat handling.
What types of products are best suited for cross-docking? +
High-velocity consumer staples (groceries, pharmaceuticals), pre-tagged retail goods, temperature-sensitive perishables, and consolidated multi-vendor LTL shipments.
