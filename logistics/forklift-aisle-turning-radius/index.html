Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard aisle width for a 4-wheel sit-down forklift? +
Standard sit-down counterbalanced forklifts handling 48x40 GMA pallets require clear rack-to-rack aisle widths of 12 to 14 feet (3.6 to 4.2 meters).
What is a Very Narrow Aisle (VNA) system? +
VNA systems utilize specialized wire-guided or rail-guided turret trucks with articulating mast heads that rotate 180 degrees, allowing pallets to be inserted into racks in aisles as narrow as 5.5 to 6.0 feet.
