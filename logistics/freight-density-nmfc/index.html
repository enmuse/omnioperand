Frequently Asked Questions

What does NMFC freight class mean in trucking? +
The National Motor Freight Traffic Association (NMFTA) classifies all freight into 18 classes (from 50 to 500) based on four transportability characteristics: density, stowability, handling, and liability.
Why does low density freight cost more to ship per pound? +
Low density freight (e.g. ping pong balls, lampshades) fills up a semi-trailer's volume ('cubes out') long before reaching the truck's 45,000-lb legal weight limit, forcing the carrier to charge higher rates per pound.
