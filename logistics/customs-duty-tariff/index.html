Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Section 321 De Minimis threshold for US imports? +
Under Section 321, shipments imported by one person on one day having a fair retail value of $800 or less enter duty-free and tax-free without formal CBP entry summary.
What is the Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF)? +
U.S. Customs and Border Protection assesses an ad valorem fee of 0.3464% on formal commercial entries to offset port processing costs, capped annually between $31.67 and $614.35.
