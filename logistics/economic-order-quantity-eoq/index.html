Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
EOQ = √[(2 · Annual Demand · Order Cost) / Annual Holding Cost per Unit]

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Unit Holding Cost H Derivation

H = Unit Cost × Carrying Cost% = $45 × 0.20 = $9.00 per unit per year.

2
EOQ Radical Derivation

EOQ = √[(2 × D × S) / H] = √[(2 × 12,000 × $75) / $9.00] = √[1,800,000 / 9] = √200,000 = 447.2 units.

3
Order Frequency Optimization

Orders per year = 12,000 / 447.2 = 26.83 orders (placing a PO roughly every 13.6 days).

4
Annual Cost Parity Invariant

At exact EOQ, Annual Ordering Cost ($75 × 26.83 = $2,012.50) exactly equals Annual Holding Cost (447.2 / 2 × $9.00 = $2,012.50). Total minimized cost = $4,025.00.

Frequently Asked Questions

What components make up inventory holding cost rate (%)? +
Holding cost rate typically comprises: cost of capital (8-12%), warehouse storage and utility rent (3-5%), insurance and taxes (2-3%), and shrinkage/obsolescence risk (3-6%), totaling 18% to 26% annually.
How do supplier MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) rules affect EOQ? +
If a factory's MOQ is 1,000 units but your EOQ is 447 units, ordering the MOQ increases your average inventory and holding cost, but may unlock volume tiered price discounts that offset the penalty.
