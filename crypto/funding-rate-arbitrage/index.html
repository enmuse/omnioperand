Frequently Asked Questions

What is Funding Rate Cash and Carry Arbitrage? +
When perpetual swap markets trade at a premium to spot, longs pay shorts funding every 8 hours. By holding spot long and shorting the perp 1x, you eliminate all price risk while collecting continuous funding yields.
What are the primary risks of funding rate arbitrage? +
Execution/slippage risk on entry, exchange counterparty risk (insolvency), funding rates turning negative (requiring position unwinding), and liquidation risk on the short leg during violent upward short-squeezes.
