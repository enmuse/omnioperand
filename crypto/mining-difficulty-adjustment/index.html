Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum allowed difficulty adjustment in Bitcoin? +
Satoshi Nakamoto built a 4x cap into the protocol: difficulty can never adjust up by more than 300% (4x) or drop by more than 75% (0.25x) in any single 2,016-block epoch to prevent catastrophic swings.
Why does difficulty adjust every 2,016 blocks? +
The 2-week adjustment mechanism ensures Bitcoin's monetary emission schedule remains mathematically deterministic regardless of how many millions of ASIC computers join or leave the network.
