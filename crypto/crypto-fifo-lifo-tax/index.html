Frequently Asked Questions

Does the IRS allow HIFO accounting for cryptocurrency? +
Yes! IRS Notice 2014-21 and Revenue Ruling 2019-24 allow specific identification of cryptocurrency tax lots, provided the taxpayer can document the date acquired, cost basis, and fair market value of each specific unit sold.
Are crypto-to-crypto swaps taxable events in the United States? +
Yes! Swapping Bitcoin for Ethereum or swapping ETH for USDC is legally treated as a property disposal at fair market value, triggering a taxable capital gain or loss event.
