Frequently Asked Questions

What is 1 Gwei in Ethereum? +
1 Gwei (giga-wei) is 10⁻⁹ ETH (0.000000001 ETH). It is the standard denomination used to quote gas prices on the Ethereum network.
Why do DEX swaps cost more gas than simple ETH transfers? +
A simple native ETH transfer between two wallets requires exactly 21,000 gas units. A DEX swap executes multiple storage read/write operations, arithmetic validations, and token allowance updates across smart contracts, consuming 130,000 to 180,000 gas units.
