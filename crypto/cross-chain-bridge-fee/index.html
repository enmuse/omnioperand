Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Burn-and-Mint and Lock-and-Mint bridges? +
Lock-and-Mint bridges lock collateral in a source chain multisig and mint wrapped tokens on the destination (high smart contract hack risk). Burn-and-Mint (like Circle CCTP) burns native USDC natively on source and mints real native USDC on destination with zero wrapper risk.
Why do cross-chain bridges get hacked so frequently? +
Bridges custody massive multi-hundred-million-dollar honey pots of locked assets secured by complex multi-chain relayer nodes, making verification oracle bugs catastrophic targets for state-sponsored exploits.
