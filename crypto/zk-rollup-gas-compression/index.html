Frequently Asked Questions

How does EIP-4844 (Blobs) reduce Layer 2 rollup fees? +
EIP-4844 introduced 'data blobs' on Ethereum that store rollup calldata off the execution layer and purge it after 18 days, reducing Layer 2 settlement costs by 80% to 95%.
What is the difference between Optimistic rollups and ZK-rollups? +
Optimistic rollups assume transactions are valid and rely on a 7-day fraud-proof challenge window for withdrawals. ZK-rollups use cryptographic validity proofs (SNARKs/STARKs) allowing mathematically instant withdrawals once the proof settles on L1.
