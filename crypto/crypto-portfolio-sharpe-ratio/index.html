Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a good Sharpe Ratio in crypto? +
A Sharpe ratio above 1.0 is considered good; above 2.0 is very good; above 3.0 is elite. Many buy-and-hold crypto portfolios exhibit Sharpe ratios between 0.6 and 1.1 due to severe 70%+ drawdowns.
Why is the Sortino Ratio often preferred over the Sharpe Ratio for crypto? +
The Sharpe Ratio penalizes upside volatility equally alongside downside crashes. The Sortino Ratio penalizes only downside volatility, giving a fairer score to asymmetric assets like Bitcoin.
