Frequently Asked Questions

Are creator royalties guaranteed on Ethereum smart contracts? +
Standard ERC-721 contracts do not enforce royalties on-chain because transfers can happen peer-to-peer. Modern collections use ERC-2981 royalty standards and programmable marketplace filter registries to block zero-royalty trading platforms.
Why did major NFT marketplaces slash creator royalties? +
In 2022-2023, zero-fee aggregators (Blur) captured 80%+ of trading volume by making royalties optional, forcing OpenSea and Magic Eden to lower default royalty enforcement to compete for pro traders.
