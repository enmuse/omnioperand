Frequently Asked Questions

How do DEX aggregators (1inch, ParaSwap) work? +
Aggregators query dozens of liquidity pools in parallel and use dynamic programming algorithms to find the mathematical path of least price resistance, splitting orders across multiple routes and hops in a single transaction.
Does split routing cost more gas? +
Yes. Splitting an order across three DEX pools requires three separate smart contract calls, increasing EVM gas by roughly 40% to 60%, but on large trades ($50k+) the slippage savings far outweigh the $5 gas difference.
