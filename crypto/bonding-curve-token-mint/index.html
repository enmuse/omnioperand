Frequently Asked Questions

How do memecoin launchpads (Pump.fun) use bonding curves? +
Launchpads use a deterministic linear bonding curve where early buyers purchase tokens cheaply from the contract. Once $69k of liquidity is deposited and the market cap reaches $69k, the curve automatically migrates and seeds a Raydium DEX pool with burned liquidity.
Does a bonding curve guarantee 100% exit liquidity? +
Yes! Because tokens are bought and sold directly against a smart contract reserve pool according to mathematical integration, there are no order books and buyers can always sell tokens back to the curve at the current marginal price.
