Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dollar-Cost Averaging better than Lump-Sum investing? +
In pure upward-trending markets, lump-sum investing mathematically outperforms ~68% of the time. However, in hyper-volatile crypto markets, DCA drastically lowers emotional stress and protects against buying market cycle tops.
What is the best frequency for crypto DCA? +
Empirical backtests show minimal difference in long-term returns between daily, weekly, and monthly DCA. Weekly DCA is widely favored because it balances smooth dollar-cost averaging with manageable exchange transaction fees.
