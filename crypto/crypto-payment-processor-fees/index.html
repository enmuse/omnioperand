Frequently Asked Questions

Can customers initiate chargebacks on cryptocurrency payments? +
No! Blockchain transactions are cryptographically final and irreversible. Once confirmed, a customer cannot call their bank to claim 'friendly fraud' or dispute the charge, saving merchants thousands in dispute fees.
How do merchants protect against crypto price volatility? +
Crypto gateways (BitPay, Coinbase Commerce) automatically convert incoming Bitcoin or Ethereum into USD stablecoins (USDC) or wire fiat dollars directly to the merchant's business bank account instantly at the moment of checkout.
