Frequently Asked Questions

What is the primary risk of stablecoin depeg arbitrage? +
Issuer insolvency risk. If the stablecoin depegs because its underlying reserves are frozen, seized, or compromised (e.g. Terra Luna algorithmic collapse or Silicon Valley Bank USDC freeze), redemptions may be halted, causing catastrophic capital loss.
Why don't retail traders capture depeg arbitrage? +
Direct 1:1 fiat redemptions at Tether or Circle require institutional verification, minimum $100,000 batch sizes, and multi-day wire settlement through traditional banking partner rails.
