OmniOperand Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes our practices concerning the collection, usage, and safeguarding of information across omnioperand.com, its programmatic APIs, and edge calculation services.

1. Zero-Account Architecture & Minimalist Collection

OmniOperand is engineered with a strict privacy-by-design architecture. Unlike legacy platforms, we do not require user registration, passwords, or personal accounts to access our interactive mathematical solvers, coordinate blueprints, or reference proofs.

All standard mathematical computations run purely client-side inside your browser engine or execute statelessly across Cloudflare edge worker isolates without retaining user session state.

2. Edge Telemetry, Logs & Cloudflare Analytics

When you access omnioperand.com, our edge network infrastructure automatically records standard diagnostic metadata necessary for network reliability, DDoS mitigation, and rate limiting:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) Address: Used strictly for transient security filtering, geo-routing to the nearest edge PoP, and automated bot prevention.
  • Browser & Operating System Headers: User-Agent strings and referrer headers utilized for responsive layout rendering and HTTP cache negotiation.
  • Privacy-First Cloudflare Web Analytics: We utilize privacy-preserving Cloudflare Web Analytics to assess aggregate page visits and Core Web Vitals. This telemetry operates without fingerprinting, does not utilize client-side tracking cookies, and does not record personally identifiable information (PII).

3. Cookies, Affiliate Attribution & Advertising Trackers

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to support essential functionality, measurement, and commercial operations:

A. Essential & Functional Cookies

We use minimal local storage and session cookies to persist your UI preferences (such as Dark/Light theme selection and unit system toggles) across page reloads.

B. Affiliate Referral Attribution Cookies

OmniOperand participates in verified affiliate marketing networks (including Impact, CJ Affiliate, and ShareASale) and partner lead programs. When you click an outbound partner recommendation link (e.g. mortgage lender comparisons, equipment dispatch, or specialized software tools), the affiliate network drops a secure, 30-day tracking cookie. This cookie tracks outbound referral conversions so that OmniOperand may earn an advertising fee or commission at zero additional cost to you.

C. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense

We may partner with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve relevant advertisements. Third-party vendors utilize cookies—including the DoubleClick DART cookie—to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other websites across the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by navigating to www.aboutads.info.

4. Global Privacy Frameworks: GDPR, CCPA & CPRA Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, OmniOperand respects statutory consumer privacy rights established by the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to request disclosure of what categories of personal information we process.
  • Right to Erasure & Deletion: You may request the deletion of any personal data submitted via contact or pre-approval inquiry forms.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: OmniOperand does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for monetary consideration. Inquiries submitted through specialized lead drawers (e.g. Halal or ITIN financing) are forwarded exclusively to the verified partner selected with your affirmative consent.
  • Non-Discrimination: You will never be denied services or charged differentiated pricing for exercising your statutory privacy rights.
