Mathematical Foundations & Decagonal Symmetries

A regular decagon is a 10-sided regular polygon with 10 reflection axes and 10-fold rotational symmetry (dihedral group D10). It is deeply intertwined with the Golden Ratio (ϕ = (1 + √5) / 2), with circumradius R strictly equal to a · ϕ.

1 Interior, Exterior & Central Angles

▼
a
Sum of Interior Angles: For n = 10:
S = (10 - 2) × 180° = 8 × 180° = 1440°   (8π rad)
b
Individual Interior Angle (α):
α = 1440° / 10 = 144°   (4π / 5 rad)
c
Central & Exterior Angle (β):
β = 360° / 10 = 36°   (π / 5 rad)
The half central angle is θ = 36° / 2 = 18°   (π / 10 rad).

2 Proof of Circumradius R = a · ϕ

▼

In the right triangle formed by the center, a vertex, and the side midpoint:

sin(18°) = (a / 2) / R ⟹ R = a / [2 · sin(18°)]

Using the exact trigonometric radical sin(18°) = (√5 - 1) / 4 = 1 / (2ϕ):

R = a / [2 · (1 / (2ϕ))] = a · ϕ = a · (1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.61803399 · a

3 Apothem (ri) & Flat-to-Flat Span (S) Derivations

▼

Applying the Pythagorean theorem to ri² + (a / 2)² = R²:

ri² = a²ϕ² - a² / 4 = a² · [(3 + √5)/2 - ¼] = a² · [(5 + 2√5) / 4]
ri = (a / 2) · √(5 + 2√5) ≈ 1.53884177 · a

The flat-to-flat span S between parallel opposite sides is twice the apothem:

S = 2ri = a · √(5 + 2√5) ≈ 3.07768354 · a

4 Area Exact Radical Derivation

▼

Partitioning into 10 central isosceles triangles:

A = 10 × (½ · a · ri) = 5a · [(a / 2)√(5 + 2√5)] = (5a² / 2) · √(5 + 2√5) ≈ 7.69420884 · a²

5 The 4 Distinct Diagonal Classes

▼

A regular decagon has 10(7) / 2 = 35 total diagonals in 4 congruence classes:

1
Short Diagonal (d1, 10 total): Spans 2 sides across 1 vertex:
d1 = a · √[(5 + √5) / 2] ≈ 1.90211303 · a
2
Medium Diagonal (d2, 10 total): Spans 3 sides across 2 vertices:
d2 = a · ϕ² = a · (3 + √5) / 2 ≈ 2.61803399 · a
3
Long Diagonal (d3, 10 total): Spans 4 sides across 3 vertices (equals flat-to-flat span S):
d3 = S = a · √(5 + 2√5) ≈ 3.07768354 · a
4
Main Diameter (d4, 5 total): Passes directly through the center connecting opposite vertices:
d4 = 2R = a · (1 + √5) ≈ 3.23606798 · a

Vanliga Frågor

What is the formula for the area of a regular decagon? +
The area of a regular decagon with side length a is A = (5/2)a² · √(5 + 2√5) ≈ 7.694209 · a². If you know the inradius (apothem) ri, the area is A = 2 · ri² · √(25 - 10√5) ≈ 3.249197 · ri².
Why is the circumradius related to the golden ratio? +
The central angle of a decagon is 36°, and sin(18°) = (√5 - 1) / 4 = 1 / (2ϕ). Since R = a / [2 sin(18°)], the circumradius simplifies exactly to R = a · ϕ = a(1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.618034a.
What are the 4 diagonal lengths of a regular decagon? +
A regular decagon has 35 diagonals in 4 distinct lengths:
  1. Short diagonal (d1): d1 = a√[(5 + √5)/2] ≈ 1.90211a
  2. Medium diagonal (d2): d2 = a · ϕ² ≈ 2.61803a
  3. Long diagonal (d3): d3 = a√(5 + 2√5) ≈ 3.07768a (equal to flat-to-flat span S)
  4. Main diameter (d4): d4 = 2R = a(1 + √5) ≈ 3.23607a
What are the interior and exterior angles of a regular decagon? +
Each interior angle of a regular decagon is (10 - 2) · 180° / 10 = 144°. Each exterior angle is 360° / 10 = 36°. The sum of all 10 interior angles is 1,440°.
