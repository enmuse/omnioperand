Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Area = Base · Height = a · b · sin(θ)

Steg-för-steg Matematiskt Bevis

1
Trigonometric Altitude and Area

Altitude h = b · sin(θ). Therefore: Area = a · h = a · b · sin(θ).

2
Law of Cosines for Diagonals

Diagonal p opposite obtuse angle (180° - θ): p = √[a² + b² + 2ab cos(θ)]. Diagonal q opposite acute angle θ: q = √[a² + b² - 2ab cos(θ)].

3
Parallelogram Law (Apollonius Identity)

Fundamental vector invariant: p² + q² = 2(a² + b²). The sum of squares of the diagonals equals the sum of squares of all four sides.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Parallelogram Law? +
The Parallelogram Law states that for any parallelogram, p² + q² = 2(a² + b²). In linear algebra and functional analysis, this identity proves whether a normed vector space is an inner product space (Hilbert space).
When does a parallelogram have maximum area? +
For fixed side lengths a and b, area is maximized when sin(θ) = 1 (θ = 90°), meaning the parallelogram becomes a rectangle.
