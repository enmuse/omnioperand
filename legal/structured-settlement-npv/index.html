Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a judge have to approve selling a structured settlement? +
Under the Structured Settlement Protection Act, state courts must review all transfers to verify that the discount rate is fair and that selling payments serves the payee's immediate financial best interests without leaving them destitute.
Are structured settlement payments tax-free? +
Yes! Under Section 104(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code, 100% of physical injury structured settlement payments (both principal and accumulated interest earnings) are exempt from federal and state income tax.
