Frequently Asked Questions

What is the elimination period in Long-Term Care insurance? +
The elimination period functions like a time-deductible. It is the number of consecutive calendar or service days (typically 90 days) you must pay for care out of pocket before insurance benefits begin paying.
What triggers long-term care insurance benefits? +
Benefits trigger when a licensed healthcare professional certifies that you cannot perform at least 2 out of 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs: bathing, dressing, eating, transferring, toileting, continence) or suffer severe cognitive impairment (Alzheimer's/dementia).
