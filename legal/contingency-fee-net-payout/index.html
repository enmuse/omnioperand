Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard contingency fee in personal injury law? +
Standard contingency contracts specify 33.3% (one-third) if settled prior to filing a lawsuit, and 40% if a lawsuit is filed or proceeds to a jury trial and appeal.
Do medical liens have to be repaid from personal injury settlements? +
Yes. Health insurers (ERISA, Medicare, Medicaid) and medical providers who treated you on a lien basis have legal subrogation rights to be repaid out of the settlement proceeds before funds are disbursed to you.
