Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Common Fund Doctrine in lien negotiation? +
The Common Fund Doctrine is a universal equitable rule establishing that when a lawyer recovers money that benefits a third-party lienholder, that lienholder must reduce its claim by its pro-rata share of attorney fees and litigation costs.
Do self-funded ERISA plans have to reduce their liens? +
Federal ERISA self-funded plans are governed by federal law (US Airways v. McCutchen). If the plan document explicitly disclaims the Common Fund and Made-Whole doctrines, ERISA plans can legally demand 100% full recovery unless negotiated down by counsel.
