Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Income Shares Model in child support? +
The Income Shares Model operates on the concept that a child should receive the same proportion of parental income that they would have received if the parents had remained living together in an intact household.
Does having more overnights reduce child support payments? +
Yes. Most states apply a parenting time cross-credit once the non-custodial parent has the children for more than 73 to 90 overnights per year, reflecting the direct expenses (food, utilities) paid during visitation.
