Frequently Asked Questions

What is a title insurance reissue rate discount? +
When refinancing or selling a property that had a title policy issued within the last 5 to 10 years, insurers offer a discounted 'reissue rate' (typically 30% to 40% off standard rates) because the title was already vetted and cleared recently.
Do I have to buy owner's title insurance when refinancing? +
No! When refinancing, you only pay for a new Lender's Title Policy to protect the new bank's first-lien mortgage. Your original Owner's Title Policy remains active for as long as you or your heirs own the home.
