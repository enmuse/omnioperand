Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between TEAS Plus and TEAS Standard? +
TEAS Plus costs $250 per class and requires choosing pre-approved goods/services descriptions from the USPTO ID Manual. TEAS Standard costs $350 per class and allows custom free-form descriptions.
What maintenance filings are required to keep a trademark active? +
Between the 5th and 6th year after registration, you must file a Section 8 Declaration of Continued Use ($225/class). Between the 9th and 10th year, you must file a combined Section 8 and Section 9 Renewal ($525/class) every 10 years indefinitely.
