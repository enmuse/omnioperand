Frequently Asked Questions

What triggers business interruption insurance coverage? +
Business interruption requires direct physical loss or damage to insured property caused by a covered peril (e.g. fire, pipe freeze, windstorm). Pure economic loss without physical damage (e.g. pandemic lockdowns) is typically excluded.
What is the Period of Restoration in business interruption? +
The period of restoration begins at the time of physical loss and ends when the property should be repaired and rebuilt with reasonable speed and similar quality.
