Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Per Diem method in a personal injury trial? +
Attorneys ask the jury to assign a daily dollar value (often the plaintiff's daily earnings, e.g. $200/day) to the physical pain and emotional suffering endured, multiplying that rate by the number of days between the crash and maximum medical recovery.
Do all states allow the Per Diem argument in court? +
Most states permit the per diem argument, but some jurisdictions (e.g. New Jersey, Pennsylvania, and federal courts) restrict attorneys from suggesting specific daily dollar figures to juries, leaving valuation strictly to jury discretion.
