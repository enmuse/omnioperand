Frequently Asked Questions

What is the legal purpose of prejudgment interest? +
Prejudgment interest compensates the plaintiff for the lost time-value of money during prolonged litigation and economically disincentivizes insurance companies from dragging out court cases to delay payment.
Does prejudgment interest apply to pain and suffering awards? +
State laws vary. In some states (e.g. New York), prejudgment interest is barred on non-economic personal injury pain and suffering damages but mandatory on property damage, wrongful death, and breach of contract.
