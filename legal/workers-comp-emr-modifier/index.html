Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Experience Modification Rate (EMR) in construction? +
An EMR is an actuarial multiplier calculated by NCCI based on a company's past 3 years of workers' comp claims. An EMR of 1.00 is average; an EMR of 1.25 increases premiums by 25%; an EMR of 0.80 discounts premiums by 20%.
Why do general contractors disqualify subcontractors with EMR above 1.0? +
Major general contractors and government agencies refuse to hire subcontractors with an EMR above 1.00 because high mods signal poor safety culture, leading to project OSHA shut-downs and heightened site liability.
