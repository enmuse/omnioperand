Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Tm = [ ΔH · 1000 / (ΔS + R · ln(C/4)) ] - 273.15 + 16.6 · log₁₀[Na_eq] | Ta = min(Tm) - 5°C

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. SantaLucia Nearest-Neighbor Model

Melting temperature is determined by enthalpy (ΔH) and entropy (ΔS) transitions between adjacent base pairs: Tm = ΔH / (ΔS + R·ln(C/4)).

2
2. Monovalent & Divalent Salt Equivalence

Mg²⁺ ions stabilize DNA phosphate backbones. Owczarzy et al. (2008) salt equivalence: [Na⁺_eq] = [Na⁺] + 120 × √([Mg²⁺] - [dNTP]).

3
3. Annealing Temperature Invariant

Optimal primer extension occurs at Ta = Tm - 5°C. For efficient PCR, the melting temperatures of the forward and reverse primers must match within ΔTm ≤ 2.0°C.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the optimal melting temperature for qPCR primers? +
The ideal qPCR primer Tm is between 58°C and 62°C (optimally 60°C). Forward and reverse primers should have melting temperatures within 1.0°C of each other to ensure uniform annealing.
Why does magnesium concentration affect primer Tm? +
Divalent magnesium cations (Mg²⁺) shield the negatively charged phosphate backbone of duplex DNA, neutralizing electrostatic repulsion and increasing the melting temperature by several degrees.
What is a GC clamp in primer design? +
A GC clamp refers to having 1 to 2 Guanine (G) or Cytosine (C) bases within the last 5 bases at the 3' end of the primer, promoting specific binding and preventing breathing during Taq polymerase initiation.
