Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the Beer-Lambert law fail at high absorbance (> 2.0)? +
At high solute concentrations (> 2.0 OD), less than 1% of light reaches the detector. Stray light and electrostatic interactions between closely packed chromophores cause deviations from linearity.
What is the standard wavelength for measuring nucleic acids and proteins? +
Nucleic acids (DNA/RNA) have peak aromatic ring absorption at 260 nm (A260). Proteins absorb at 280 nm (A280) due to tryptophan, tyrosine, and phenylalanine residues.
