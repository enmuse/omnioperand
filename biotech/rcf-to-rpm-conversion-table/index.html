Centrifuge g to RPM Conversion Table & Protocol Nomogram

Convert between rotational speed (RPM) and Relative Centrifugal Force (RCF in ×g) using exact rotor radius geometry to reproduce centrifugation protocols.

Direct Answer • RCF (×g) to RPM Inversion

To convert between RCF (×g) and RPM, use the standard laboratory formula: RCF = 1.118 × 10⁻⁵ · r · RPM² or solve for RPM: RPM = √[RCF / (1.118 × 10⁻⁵ · r)], where r is the rotor radius in centimeters. For an 8.5 cm benchtop rotor: 300 ×g = 1,777 RPM, 1,000 ×g = 3,244 RPM, 3,000 ×g = 5,619 RPM, and 10,000 ×g = 10,259 RPM.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Centrifugal Acceleration Formula

Centrifugal acceleration a = ω² · r, where angular velocity ω = 2π · RPM / 60. Normalizing to Earth gravity (g = 9.80665 m/s²) yields the constant 1.118 × 10⁻⁵.

2
RCF Calculation

RCF = 1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × Radius (8.5 cm) × (14,000 RPM)² = 1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × 8.5 × 196,000,000 = 18,624.76 ≈ 18,625 ×g.

3
Peripheral Speed

Linear Velocity v = (2π · 8.5 cm / 100) × (14,000 / 60) = 0.534 m × 233.33 rev/s = 124.6 m/s (448.6 km/h).

4
Protocol Reproducibility Rule

Scientific protocols must always specify g-force (RCF), never RPM alone. Running 14,000 RPM on a larger 12 cm rotor produces 26,295 ×g (+41% higher force), potentially lysing delicate organelles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert RCF (×g) to RPM? +
To convert RCF (relative centrifugal force) to RPM, use the inverted formula: RPM = √[RCF / (1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × r)], where r is the rotor radius in centimeters. For an 8.5 cm rotor, 300 ×g equals 1,777 RPM, 1,000 ×g equals 3,244 RPM, and 10,000 ×g equals 10,259 RPM.
What is 300 xg to RPM? +
For a standard 8.5 cm benchtop rotor, 300 ×g corresponds to approximately 1,777 RPM. For a 10 cm rotor, 300 ×g is 1,638 RPM; for a 12 cm rotor, it is 1,496 RPM.
What is 1000 xg to RPM? +
For a standard 8.5 cm benchtop rotor, 1,000 ×g corresponds to approximately 3,244 RPM. For a 10 cm rotor, 1,000 ×g is 2,991 RPM; for a 12 cm rotor, it is 2,731 RPM.
Why is it dangerous to report centrifugation speeds in RPM? +
RPM describes only how fast the rotor spins, ignoring the physical size of the rotor. Two centrifuges running at the same 5,000 RPM will exert drastically different forces if one has an 8 cm rotor (2,236 ×g) and the other has a 16 cm rotor (4,472 ×g).
What is the difference between r_min, r_av, and r_max in centrifuge rotors? +
r_min is the distance from the spindle to the liquid surface, r_max is the distance to the tube tip, and r_av is the midpoint. RCF is typically reported at r_max (pellet force).
