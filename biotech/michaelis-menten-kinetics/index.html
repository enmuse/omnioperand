Frequently Asked Questions

What does a low Km signify in enzyme kinetics? +
A low Km indicates high enzyme affinity for its substrate, meaning very little substrate is required to achieve half-maximal catalytic velocity.
How does a competitive inhibitor alter Km and Vmax? +
A competitive inhibitor increases apparent Km (requiring more substrate to reach half-speed) but does not change Vmax, because high substrate concentrations displace the inhibitor.
