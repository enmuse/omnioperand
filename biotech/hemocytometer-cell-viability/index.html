Frequently Asked Questions

What is the optimal cell count per large square on a hemocytometer? +
For maximum statistical precision without counting fatigue, aim for 20 to 50 cells per 1 mm² large square. If squares contain > 100 cells, dilute the sample; if < 15 cells, concentrate the cell pellet.
Why must Trypan blue counting be completed within 3 to 5 minutes? +
Trypan blue is cytotoxic over extended exposure. After 10-15 minutes, viable cells begin absorbing the dye, creating false non-viable readings.
