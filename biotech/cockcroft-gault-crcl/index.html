Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Numerator Product

Numerator = (140 - Age) × Weight (kg) = (140 - 65) × 72.0 = 75 × 72.0 = 5,400.

2
Denominator Serum Creatinine Product

Denominator = 72 × Serum Creatinine (1.40 mg/dL) = 100.8.

3
Base Male Creatinine Clearance

CrCl = 5,400 / 100.8 = 53.57 mL/min (if female, multiply by 0.85 = 45.54 mL/min).

4
Clinical Dosing Classification

A CrCl of 53.6 mL/min indicates moderate renal impairment (Stage 3). Common renally cleared antibiotics (e.g. Vancomycin, Levofloxacin, Cefepime) require extended dosing intervals or 25-50% dose reductions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the FDA mandate Cockcroft-Gault over eGFR (CKD-EPI) for drug dosing? +
Most pharmaceutical clinical trials and drug package inserts historically established renal dosage adjustments using Cockcroft-Gault CrCl (mL/min) rather than body-surface-adjusted eGFR (mL/min/1.73m²).
When should Ideal Body Weight (IBW) be used instead of actual weight? +
In significantly obese patients (BMI ≥ 30 kg/m²), using actual weight overestimates renal clearance because adipose tissue does not produce creatinine. Clinicians typically substitute Ideal Body Weight (IBW) or Adjusted Body Weight (AdjBW).
