Governing Mathematical Formula:
Area A = π · (D / 2)² | Pusher Velocity v = Flow Rate Q / Area A | Duration = Volume / Q

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Plunger Cross-Sectional Area

The inner barrel diameter D determines cylinder area: A = π × (D/2)². For a 10 mL BD syringe (D = 14.48 mm), area A = 164.68 mm².

2
2. Flow Rate to Linear Speed Conversion

Syringe pump leadscrews drive the plunger linearly. 1 µL equals 1 mm³ of volume. Linear pusher velocity is v (mm/min) = Q (mm³/min) / Area (mm²).

3
3. Infusion Time Invariant

Total infusion duration is simply Volume / Flow Rate. For microfluidic droplet generation or microdialysis, matching linear speed to microchannel velocity prevents cell shear stress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a smaller syringe give smoother microfluidic flow? +
A smaller syringe diameter has a much smaller cross-sectional area, meaning the motor leadscrew must turn much faster to deliver the same volume, eliminating discrete motor stepping pulsations.
Where do you find syringe inner barrel diameters? +
Inner barrel diameters are published by manufacturers (BD, Hamilton, Terumo) and ISO 7886 standards, ranging from 1.46 mm for 100µL syringes up to 26.7 mm for 60mL syringes.
How do you convert µL/min to mL/hr? +
Multiply µL/min by 60 to find µL/hr, then divide by 1,000 to find mL/hr (Formula: mL/hr = µL/min × 0.06).
