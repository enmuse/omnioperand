Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between clearance and half-life? +
Clearance (CL) is the true physiological organ capacity (liver and kidneys) to remove drug from the blood per unit time. Half-life (t½) is a dependent hybrid parameter determined by both clearance and how widely the drug distributes throughout tissue (Vd).
How many half-lives until a drug is completely gone from the body? +
After 1 half-life, 50% remains; after 3.3 half-lives, 90% is cleared; after 5 half-lives, 97% is cleared; and after 7 half-lives, 99.2% is eliminated.
