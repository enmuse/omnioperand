Frequently Asked Questions

Can Volume of Distribution exceed total human body volume? +
Yes! Highly lipophilic drugs (like Chloroquine with a Vd of ~15,000 L, or Amiodarone with ~5,000 L) bind extensively to adipose tissue and organs, leaving almost zero drug in the bloodstream. Because Vd = Dose / Blood Conc, an extremely tiny blood concentration produces an apparent Vd thousands of liters larger than the human body.
Why does clearance NOT affect the loading dose? +
Loading dose depends entirely on Volume of Distribution (filling the tank), not clearance. Clearance only dictates the maintenance infusion rate (replenishing what leaks out).
