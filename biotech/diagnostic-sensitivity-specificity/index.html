Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
Sensitivity = TP / (TP + FN) | Specificity = TN / (TN + FP) | PPV = TP / (TP + FP)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Total Gold-Standard Disease Population

Total Diseased = TP (95) + FN (5) = 100 patients. Total Non-Diseased = FP (10) + TN (890) = 900 healthy individuals.

2
Intrinsic Test Accuracy (Sensitivity & Specificity)

Sensitivity = TP / (TP + FN) = 95 / 100 = 95.0%. Specificity = TN / (TN + FP) = 890 / 900 = 98.89% ≈ 98.9%.

3
Positive Predictive Value (PPV)

Total Positive Tests = TP (95) + FP (10) = 105. PPV = 95 / 105 = 90.48% ≈ 90.5% probability a positive patient truly has the disease.

4
Negative Predictive Value (NPV)

Total Negative Tests = TN (890) + FN (5) = 895. NPV = 890 / 895 = 99.44% probability a negative test is genuinely healthy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does disease prevalence affect PPV and NPV? +
Sensitivity and specificity are intrinsic properties of the assay that do not change with prevalence. However, in low-prevalence screening populations (e.g. 0.1%), even a 99% specific test will produce more false positives than true positives, dramatically lowering PPV.
What is the clinical heuristic 'SnNout' and 'SpPin'? +
A highly Sensitive test, when Negative, rules OUT disease (SnNout). A highly Specific test, when Positive, rules IN disease (SpPin).
