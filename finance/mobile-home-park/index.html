Frequently Asked Questions

Why do mobile home parks have the lowest tenant turnover in real estate? +
Moving a mobile home costs $5,000 to $10,000 in transport permits, teardown, and setup fees. Because homes are expensive to relocate, average tenant tenure in a well-managed MHP exceeds 10 to 15 years.
Why are new mobile home parks almost never built? +
Zoning restrictions. Municipalities almost universally refuse to grant new zoning permits for mobile home parks due to NIMBY opposition, making existing licensed parks grandfathered, non-replicable monopolies.
