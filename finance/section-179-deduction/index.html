The Section 179 Expensing Rule

Section 179 allows businesses to write off 100% of the purchase price of qualifying equipment in the year placed in service, rather than depreciating it over 5 to 7 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a financed or leased piece of equipment qualify for Section 179? +
YES! You can finance 100% of an equipment purchase with $0 down and still claim the full Section 179 tax deduction in Year 1, creating a cash-flow surplus where tax savings exceed your initial lease payments.
Can Section 179 create a net operating loss (NOL)? +
No. Section 179 cannot exceed your active business taxable income. Any unused Section 179 deduction above net income carries forward to future tax years. (Bonus depreciation, however, CAN create a tax loss).
