Frequently Asked Questions

What is Carried Interest (Promote) in private equity real estate? +
Promote is a performance fee paid to the General Partner sponsor. It is a share of project profits (typically 20%) distributed above an agreed preferred return hurdle, incentivizing sponsors to outperform return targets.
What is a clawback provision in a real estate waterfall? +
A clawback clause mandates that if a sponsor takes promote payments in early years, but the deal ultimately underperforms over the full hold period, the sponsor must return excess promote to ensure LPs achieve their preferred return.
