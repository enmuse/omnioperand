Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

Gross vs Net Burn Differential

Runway Derivation

Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a safe cash runway for startups? +
Early-stage startups should maintain 18 to 24 months of cash runway. Having under 6 months of runway significantly weakens your negotiating leverage with investors.
How can a startup extend runway without raising capital? +
Founders can extend runway by cutting non-essential SaaS tooling, freezing non-revenue headcount, renegotiating cloud hosting commitments, and converting customers to upfront annual billing.
