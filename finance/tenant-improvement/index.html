Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between turnkey buildout and a TI allowance? +
A TI allowance provides a fixed dollar budget (e.g. $45/SF); any cost overruns must be paid by the tenant. In a turnkey buildout, the landlord agrees to deliver the space completed to agreed architectural specifications regardless of cost overruns.
Can unused TI allowance be taken as rent credit? +
Only if explicitly negotiated in the commercial lease agreement. Most standard commercial leases stipulate that unused TI allowances are forfeited by the tenant at rent commencement.
