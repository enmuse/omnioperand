Governing Mathematical Formula:
LTV:CAC = Customer Lifetime Value / CAC | Payback Months = CAC / (ARPA × Gross Margin %)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Unit Economic Multiplier

LTV:CAC Ratio = LTV / CAC. Benchmarks: < 1.0 = Value destructive; 1.0–2.9 = Sub-optimal margin; 3.0–5.0 = Gold standard scalable growth; > 5.0 = Under-investing in acquisition.

2
Cash Flow Payback Period

Payback Period in Months = CAC / (ARPA × Gross Margin%). Measures how many months of customer subscription cash flow are required to recover upfront marketing and sales cash outflows.

3
Net Economic Profit Contribution

Net Capital Surplus = LTV - CAC. Represents net scalable profit returned per customer account.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ideal CAC payback period for SaaS? +
Top-quartile venture-backed B2B SaaS companies target a CAC payback period between 8 and 12 months. Payback longer than 18 months creates severe working capital constraints and cash drag.
Can an LTV:CAC ratio be too high? +
Yes! An LTV:CAC ratio above 5:1 or 6:1 usually indicates that you are under-spending on sales and marketing, leaving significant market share on the table for competitors.
