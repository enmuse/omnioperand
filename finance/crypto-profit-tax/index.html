Frequently Asked Questions

Is swapping one crypto for another taxable? +
Yes! Swapping Bitcoin for Ethereum or a stablecoin is treated by the IRS as selling Bitcoin at fair market value and immediately purchasing the second asset, triggering a capital gain or loss.
