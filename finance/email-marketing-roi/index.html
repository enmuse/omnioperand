Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered an average email marketing ROI? +
According to the Data & Marketing Association (DMA), email marketing averages a 3,600% to 4,200% ROI ($36 to $42 return per $1 spent), outperforming social media and paid search.
What is Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)? +
CTOR measures clicks divided by opens (rather than total delivered emails), isolating how compelling your email body copy and call-to-action were to people who actually opened the email.
