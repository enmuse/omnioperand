Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in commercial CAM expenses? +
Common Area Maintenance (CAM) includes parking lot sweeping, snow removal, exterior landscaping, security, roof maintenance, HVAC service contracts, and property management administrative fees.
What is an expense stop in a modified gross lease? +
An expense stop sets a baseline year of operating expenses paid by the landlord. If expenses rise in subsequent years above that stop, the tenant pays the pro-rata increase.
