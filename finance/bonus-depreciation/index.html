Frequently Asked Questions

What assets qualify for Section 168(k) bonus depreciation? +
Qualifying assets must have a recovery period of 20 years or less, including machinery, computers, appliances, office furniture, qualified improvement property (QIP), and heavy commercial vehicles over 6,000 lbs GVWR.
Can bonus depreciation create a tax loss? +
YES! Unlike Section 179 which cannot exceed net business income, bonus depreciation can create a Net Operating Loss (NOL) that can offset up to 80% of taxable income in future years.
