Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to HSA funds after age 65? +
After age 65, you can withdraw HSA funds for ANY non-medical purpose penalty-free, paying only ordinary income tax (identical to a traditional IRA). For medical expenses, withdrawals remain 100% tax-free forever.
Is there a deadline to reimburse myself for medical expenses from an HSA? +
No! There is zero time limit under IRS rules. You can incur a medical expense at age 30, keep the receipt, and reimburse yourself tax-free at age 65 after the funds have compounded for 35 years.
