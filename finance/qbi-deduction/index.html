The 20% Pass-Through Deduction Rule

Taxpayers operating sole proprietorships, partnerships, or S-Corps can deduct up to 20% of net qualified business income directly off their personal tax return.

2
SSTB Threshold Limits

For 2024, the full 20% deduction is available to all businesses below taxable income thresholds ($191,950 Single / $383,900 MFJ).

3
High-Income Phase-Out Restrictions

Above the threshold, Specified Service Trades (doctors, lawyers, accountants, consultants) completely phase out. Non-service businesses are limited by W-2 wages paid.

Frequently Asked Questions

What businesses are classified as SSTBs under Section 199A? +
Specified Service Trades or Businesses include healthcare (doctors, dentists), law, accounting, actuarial science, performing arts, consulting, athletics, financial services, and brokerage services. Engineers and architects are explicitly excluded from SSTB restrictions.
Does rental real estate qualify for the 20% QBI deduction? +
Yes! Rental real estate qualifies under IRS safe harbor Rev. Proc. 2019-38 if you maintain separate books, perform at least 250 hours of rental services annually, and maintain contemporaneous service logs.
