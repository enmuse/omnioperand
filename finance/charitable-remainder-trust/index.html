Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a CRUT and a CRAT? +
A Charitable Remainder Unitrust (CRUT) pays a fixed percentage of trust assets revalued annually (income grows with market performance). A Charitable Remainder Annuity Trust (CRAT) pays a fixed dollar amount annually regardless of market performance.
Can I replace the wealth given to charity for my children? +
Yes! Many families pair a CRUT with a Wealth Replacement Trust (Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust - ILIT), using a portion of the annual income stream to fund life insurance that passes completely tax-free to children.
