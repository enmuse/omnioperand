Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to commercial property value when cap rates rise? +
Rising cap rates decrease property values unless NOI increases fast enough to offset the expansion. For a property with $100k NOI, a cap rate increase from 5% to 6% cuts property value from $2,000,000 down to $1,666,667 (a $333,333 loss).
What causes cap rates to compress or expand? +
Cap rates compress (lowering yields, raising valuations) during periods of low interest rates, high institutional capital inflows, and strong rent growth. Cap rates expand when borrowing interest rates rise.
