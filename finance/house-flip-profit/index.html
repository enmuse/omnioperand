Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 70% rule in real estate? +
The 70% rule states that an investor should pay no more than 70% of the property's After Repair Value (ARV) minus repair costs. It guarantees adequate profit margin after accounting for financing and resale commissions.
Can you use the 70% rule in competitive high-price markets? +
In expensive coastal markets (San Diego, Seattle), flippers often compress margins to a 75% or 80% rule because fixed percentage fees translate into higher absolute dollar profits on $1M+ homes.
