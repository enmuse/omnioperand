Frequently Asked Questions

What is estate tax portability? +
Portability allows a surviving spouse to elect to transfer any unused estate tax exemption from their deceased spouse by filing IRS Form 706 within 5 years of death, effectively doubling the household exemption.
How does the annual gift exclusion work? +
In 2024, you can gift up to $18,000 per recipient ($36,000 for married couples) to an unlimited number of people without reporting it to the IRS or reducing your lifetime estate tax exemption.
