Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the stock price drops after my RSUs vest? +
You owe taxes based on the fair market value on the exact day of vesting. If the stock drops subsequently, you realize a capital loss when selling, but your W-2 taxable compensation for the vesting year remains unchanged.
