Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Six Pillars of Vehicle Cost Breakdown

TCO = 1) Depreciation (typically 60% drop in 5 yrs) + 2) Fuel (60 mo) + 3) Insurance (60 mo) + 4) Financing Interest + 5) Maintenance & Repairs ($1,000/yr) + 6) Registration/Fees ($300/yr).

2
Hidden Depreciation Primacy

Depreciation is the single largest hidden expense of vehicle ownership, frequently exceeding fuel and insurance combined.

3
Per-Mile Amortization Metric

Total 5-year miles = 5 × 13,500 = 67,500 miles. Cost per Mile = TCO / 67,500.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the true cost of owning an average new car? +
According to AAA's annual 'Your Driving Costs' study, the average cost to own and operate a new car in the United States exceeds $12,000 per year ($1,000+ per month or ~$0.75 to $0.85 per mile) when including all depreciation and insurance.
How can I cut my vehicle TCO by half? +
Buy reliable 4-year-old used vehicles in cash, shop auto insurance annually, maintain vehicles proactively to prevent catastrophic repairs, and hold cars for 8 to 10 years.
