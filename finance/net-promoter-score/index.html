Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
NPS = % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Response Partitioning

Total Respondents N = Promoters + Passives + Detractors.

2
Bain & Company NPS Formulation

NPS = [(Promoters / N) × 100] - [(Detractors / N) × 100]. Passives count toward total population N, thereby diluting the percentage.

3
Score Range Interpretation

NPS ranges from -100 to +100. > 0 is good, > +50 is excellent, and > +70 is world-class customer loyalty (Apple, Costco tier).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a good Net Promoter Score for B2B SaaS? +
For B2B software companies, an NPS above +30 is considered good, above +50 is excellent, and above +70 represents best-in-class product-led growth.
Why are ratings 7 and 8 excluded from the subtraction? +
Passives (7-8) are satisfied but not enthusiastic enough to proactively recommend your product. They count in the total denominator, naturally moderating the score.
