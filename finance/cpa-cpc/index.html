Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
CPA = Cost Per Click (CPC) / Conversion Rate %

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Traffic Funnel Mathematical Coupling

Conversions = Clicks × Conversion Rate%. Total Ad Spend = Clicks × CPC.

2
CPA Algebraic Derivation

CPA = Total Spend / Conversions = (Clicks × CPC) / (Clicks × CVR) = CPC / CVR.

3
Optimization Leverage

Doubling conversion rate from 2% to 4% cuts your CPA in half without changing ad auction bids.
