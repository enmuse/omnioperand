Frequently Asked Questions

Should I sell or hold my RSUs after they vest? +
Holding vested company stock is mathematically identical to receiving a cash bonus and buying company stock on the open market. Unless you would proactively buy your company stock with cash, selling immediately to diversify into broad market index funds eliminates concentrated single-stock risk.
What is the tax basis of vested RSUs? +
Your cost basis is the fair market value on the date of vesting. If you sell immediately upon vesting, your capital gain is approximately $0.
