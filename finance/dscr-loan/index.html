Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Net Operating Income

NOI = Gross Rental Revenue - Operating Expenses (Taxes, Insurance, HOA).

2

DSCR = NOI / Monthly Debt Service (P&I). Ratios >= 1.25 qualify for prime pricing.
