Frequently Asked Questions

What do hard money lenders look for during approval? +
Unlike traditional mortgage banks that scrutinize personal tax returns and W-2 paystubs, hard money lenders focus on the asset's After Repair Value (ARV), the borrower's track record of successful flips, and sufficient equity cushion (typically 65-75% ARV limit).
Can hard money points be rolled into the loan balance? +
Many hard money lenders allow points, processing fees, and even an interest reserve to be financed directly into the total loan balance, minimizing out-of-pocket cash required at closing.
