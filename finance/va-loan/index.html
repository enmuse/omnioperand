Frequently Asked Questions

Who is exempt from the VA funding fee? +
Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, surviving spouses eligible for Dependency and Indemnity Compensation (DIC), and active-duty Purple Heart recipients are exempt from paying the funding fee.
Is there a maximum limit on a VA loan? +
For veterans with full entitlement, there is no conforming loan limit; borrowers can obtain 0% down financing up to whatever loan amount their income qualifies for.
