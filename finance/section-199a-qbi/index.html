Governing Mathematical Formula:
Deduction = min[ 20% · QBI, max(50% · W-2 Wages, 25% · W-2 + 2.5% · UBIA) ]

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. 20% Tentative Pass-Through Base

The preliminary deduction equals 20% of net Qualified Business Income from eligible domestic trades or businesses.

2
2. Statutory Phaseout Brackets

If taxable income is below $191,950 (Single) or $383,900 (MFJ), the full 20% deduction is allowed regardless of W-2 wages, UBIA property, or SSTB status.

3
3. Wage and Capital Limitation Tests

Above the phase-out limit, the deduction is capped at the greater of: (a) 50% of W-2 wages, or (b) 25% of W-2 wages plus 2.5% of UBIA property. SSTBs receive $0 deduction above the upper threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions

What businesses qualify for the 20% QBI deduction? +
Sole proprietorships, partnerships, LLCs, and S-corporations generating domestic business profit qualify. C-corporations and employee wages do not qualify.
What is an SSTB under Section 199A? +
A Specified Service Trade or Business (SSTB) is any business involving the performance of services in health, law, accounting, actuarial science, performing arts, consulting, athletics, financial services, or brokerage services.
What are the 2024/2026 QBI income thresholds? +
For Single filers, the phase-out range is $191,950 to $241,950. For Married Filing Jointly filers, the phase-out range is $383,900 to $483,900.
