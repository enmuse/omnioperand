Governing Mathematical Formula:
Blended APR = [(First Balance × First Rate) + (Second Balance × Second Rate)] / Total Debt

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Debt Balance Weighting

Total Debt = 1st Mortgage ($320,000) + 2nd Mortgage ($80,000) = $400,000. 1st Mortgage represents 80% of total debt; 2nd represents 20%.

2
Blended Rate Equation

Blended APR = (0.80 × 3.25%) + (0.20 × 9.50%) = 2.60% + 1.90% = 4.50%.

3
Preserving Low-Rate Primary Debt

Borrowing $80,000 at a steep 9.5% HELOC rate creates a blended rate of only 4.50%, far superior to refinancing the entire $400,000 at a current 6.8% cash-out mortgage rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is keeping a low-rate first mortgage and taking a HELOC better than a cash-out refinance? +
If you have a 3% mortgage, a cash-out refinance wipes out that 3% rate across your ENTIRE balance. Taking a 9% HELOC for only the incremental cash you need keeps your blended borrowing cost significantly lower.
What is a wraparound mortgage? +
A wraparound mortgage is a form of creative seller financing where the seller keeps their existing low-interest mortgage in place while extending a larger loan to the buyer at a higher rate, profiting from the spread between the two rates.
