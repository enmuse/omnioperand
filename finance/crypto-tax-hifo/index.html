Analytical Derivation & Methodology

1
1. IRS Specific Identification Guidance

Under IRS Revenue Ruling 2019-24, cryptocurrency investors who maintain detailed records (transaction IDs, timestamps, cost basis) are legally permitted to use Specific Identification, including HIFO (Highest-In, First-Out).

2
2. HIFO vs FIFO Tax Mechanics

FIFO (First-In, First-Out) assumes the earliest coins acquired are sold first, which typically results in the lowest cost basis and largest taxable capital gain. HIFO matches sales against the highest acquisition prices first, immediately maximizing cost basis deductions and generating tax-loss harvesting benefits.

3
3. Holding Period Bifurcation

Assets held 365 days or less are classified as short-term capital gains and taxed as ordinary income (up to 37% federal). Assets held greater than 365 days receive preferential long-term capital gains tax rates (0%, 15%, or 20%).
