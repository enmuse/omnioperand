Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if a property stabilizes before the interest reserve is exhausted? +
Unused interest reserve funds are not kept by the lender. When the bridge loan is paid off through a permanent refinance or sale, any remaining balance in the interest reserve escrow reduces the final loan payoff balance dollar-for-dollar.
What is an interest rate cap in commercial bridge lending? +
Because bridge loans have floating interest rates, lenders mandate that borrowers purchase an Interest Rate Cap (derivative hedge) that sets a maximum ceiling on SOFR to protect against default during rate spikes.
