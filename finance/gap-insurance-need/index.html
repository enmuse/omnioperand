Governing Mathematical Formula:
GAP Exposure = Outstanding Loan Balance - Vehicle Actual Cash Value (ACV)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Total Loss Payout Rule

If a vehicle is totaled or stolen, standard auto insurance pays ONLY Actual Cash Value (ACV) minus your deductible, NOT what you owe on the loan.

2
The Negative Equity Gap

GAP Shortfall = Loan Payoff - (ACV - Deductible). If you owe $32,000 and ACV is $25,500, you must write a $7,000 check to the lender for a car that no longer exists.

3
Purchasing Heuristic

GAP insurance is essential whenever loan-to-value (LTV) exceeds 105-110%, typically occurring on loans with under 15% down payment or terms over 60 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my car is totaled and I do not have GAP insurance? +
Your auto insurer pays market value to the lender. The remaining balance (frequently $3,000 to $8,000+) remains your personal legal debt, which the bank will demand in full immediately.
Where should I buy GAP insurance? +
Never buy GAP from the dealership (where it costs $800 to $1,200). Buy it from your primary auto insurance company (State Farm, Geico, Progressive), where it typically costs only $20 to $40 per year.
