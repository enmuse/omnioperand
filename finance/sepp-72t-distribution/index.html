Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

The 10% Early Penalty Waiver

Withdrawals from IRAs before age 59½ normally trigger a 10% early withdrawal excise tax penalty. Section 72(t) waives the penalty if payments are substantially equal.

2
IRS Approved Calculation Methods

Three calculation methods exist: 1) Required Minimum Distribution (RMD - variable), 2) Fixed Amortization (highest payout), and 3) Fixed Annuitization.

3
Strict Commitment Lock-In

Once started, payments must continue without modification for the LONGER of 5 full years or until you reach age 59½. Any modification retroactively triggers all waived 10% penalties plus interest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you change the withdrawal amount once a 72(t) SEPP starts? +
No! If you alter the withdrawal amount by even $1 before age 59½ (or 5 years), the IRS busts the plan, demanding retroactive 10% penalties plus interest on every single distribution taken since inception.
Can I split my IRA before starting a 72(t) plan? +
YES! This is a premier wealth planning strategy: roll the exact amount needed for your desired annual income into a separate IRA and establish the 72(t) on only that specific account, leaving other IRAs untouched.
