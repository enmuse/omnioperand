Frequently Asked Questions

Do all states allow a sales tax credit for vehicle trade-ins? +
Most states do, but California, Hawaii, Kentucky, Maryland, Michigan, Montana, and Virginia do not allow full trade-in deductions or have specific state restrictions.
Should I sell my car privately or trade it in to save tax? +
If private sale market value exceeds dealer trade-in offer by more than the sales tax savings (e.g. more than 7-8%), private sale yields more cash even after paying full tax on the new vehicle.
