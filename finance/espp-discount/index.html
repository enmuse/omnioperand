The Lookback Provision Super-Power

With a lookback provision, your 15% discount applies to the LOWER of the start price ($100) or end price ($120): Purchase Price = min($100, $120) × 85% = $85.00.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a qualifying and disqualifying disposition? +
A qualifying disposition requires holding shares for 2 years from the offering date and 1 year from the purchase date, granting favorable capital gains rates. In a disqualifying disposition (selling immediately), the 15% discount is taxed as ordinary income, but eliminates all market downside risk.
Should I sell my ESPP shares immediately upon purchase? +
Yes! Selling immediately locks in an immediate, risk-free 17.6% to 40%+ return without taking on single-stock volatility.
