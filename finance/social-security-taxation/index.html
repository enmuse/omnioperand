Frequently Asked Questions

Are Social Security tax thresholds adjusted for inflation? +
NO! The thresholds ($25k Single / $32k MFJ) were established in 1983 and were deliberately never indexed to inflation. As a result, while only 10% of retirees paid tax in 1984, over 55% of retirees pay tax on their benefits today.
Do states tax Social Security benefits? +
Most states (38 states plus DC) do not tax Social Security benefits. Only 10 states (Colorado, Connecticut, Minnesota, Montana, New Mexico, Rhode Island, Utah, Vermont, West Virginia, and Missouri) tax a portion of benefits under specific state rules.
