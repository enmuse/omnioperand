Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Maximum Statutory Credit

Base Credit = $2,000 per qualifying child under age 17. For 2 children: 2 × $2,000 = $4,000.

2
High-Income Phase-Out Thresholds

The credit phases out by $50 for every $1,000 (or fraction thereof) that MAGI exceeds $200,000 (Single / Head of Household) or $400,000 (Married Filing Jointly).

3
Refundable Additional Child Tax Credit (ACTC)

Up to $1,700 per child (2024 limit) is refundable even if your total income tax liability is zero, calculated as 15% of earned income above $2,500.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a tax credit and a tax deduction? +
A tax deduction reduces taxable income (e.g. a $2,000 deduction at a 24% tax rate saves $480 in taxes). A tax credit reduces your actual tax bill dollar-for-dollar (a $2,000 tax credit saves a full $2,000 in cash).
At what income does the Child Tax Credit phase out completely? +
For married couples with 2 children, the credit begins phasing out at $400,000 MAGI and is completely eliminated at $480,000 MAGI.
