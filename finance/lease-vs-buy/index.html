Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Gross Capital Outflow Modeling

Leasing Outflow = Down Payment + (Monthly Lease × Term) + Disposition Fee ($400). Loan Outflow = Down Payment + (Monthly Payment × Term).

2
Residual Equity Valuation

At month 36, the lessee walks away with $0 equity. The purchaser retains an asset worth: Equity = Vehicle Price × (Resale Value% / 100).

3
Net Economic Depreciation Cost

Net Purchase Cost = Total Loan Cash Outflows - Residual Equity Asset Value. Buying almost always beats leasing unless the vehicle depreciates catastrophically faster than manufacturer residual estimates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it financially better to lease or buy a car? +
From a pure wealth-building standpoint, buying and holding a vehicle for 6 to 10 years is vastly superior. Leasing provides lower monthly payments and warranty peace of mind, but costs significantly more over time because you pay the steepest part of the depreciation curve repeatedly.
What is a car lease Money Factor? +
Money factor is lease interest expressed in decimal form. Multiply the money factor by 2,400 to find the equivalent annual percentage rate (APR). For example: 0.0025 × 2,400 = 6.0% APR.
