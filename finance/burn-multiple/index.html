Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Bessemer Unit Capital Burn Metric

Burn Multiple = Net Burn / Net New ARR. Unlike CAC, which only measures sales and marketing, the Burn Multiple includes all corporate expenses (R&D, G&A, management, cloud infrastructure).

2
Venture Capital Benchmark Thresholds

< 1.0× = Amazing (Best-in-class); 1.0×–1.5× = Good; 1.5×–2.0× = Suspect (Needs optimization); > 2.0× = Bad (Burning too much cash for slow growth).

3
Macro Cycle Significance

In zero-interest-rate environments, investors tolerated 2.5×+ multiples. In modern disciplined capital markets, sub-1.5× multiples are required for premium venture valuations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Burn Multiple formula? +
Burn Multiple = Net Burn / Net New ARR. For example, if a startup burns $3 million to generate $2 million in new ARR, its Burn Multiple is 1.5×.
How does the Burn Multiple differ from CAC Payback? +
CAC payback only tracks sales and marketing spend. The Burn Multiple is comprehensive—capturing engineering salaries, rent, legal fees, server costs, and churn drag.
