Frequently Asked Questions

Who created the SaaS Magic Number? +
The SaaS Magic Number was popularized by venture capital firm Scale Venture Partners as a standard metric to evaluate when a software company is ready to scale go-to-market spending.
Why does the formula use the previous quarter's sales & marketing spend? +
Sales cycles in B2B require time; marketing spend in quarter Q-1 typically generates closed deals that show up as recognized revenue in quarter Q.
