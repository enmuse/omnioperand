Governing Mathematical Formula:
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Customer Lifespan Geometric Series

Assuming a constant monthly churn rate c, average customer lifespan in months is T = 1 / c (e.g. 1 / 0.025 = 40 months).

2
Gross Margin Adjusted Revenue Contribution

Net revenue contribution per month M = ARPA · (Gross Margin% / 100).

3
Lifetime Economic Value

LTV = M · T = [ARPA · Gross Margin%] / Churn. Gross margin adjustment is mandatory to prevent inflating lifetime value with delivery cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is gross margin required when calculating LTV? +
Calculating LTV on top-line revenue without gross margin ignores hosting, customer support, and payment processing costs, falsely inflating how much you can afford to spend on acquisition.
What is a healthy monthly churn rate for B2B SaaS? +
For enterprise SaaS ($25k+ ACV), monthly churn should be under 0.5% to 1.0% (net negative churn). For mid-market SaaS, 1.0% to 2.0% is typical. For SMB SaaS, 2.5% to 4.0% is common.
