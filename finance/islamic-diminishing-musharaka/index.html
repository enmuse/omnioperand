Frequently Asked Questions

How does Diminishing Musharaka differ from a conventional interest-bearing mortgage?

In a Diminishing Musharaka (Musharaka Mutanaqisa), the bank and homebuyer enter a joint co-ownership partnership (Shirkat-ul-Milk). The buyer pays fair market rent on the bank's portion while simultaneously purchasing units of bank equity each month until 100% legal title transfers to the homeowner. There is zero compounding interest or penalty usury (Riba).

What governing standards validate this financing as Shariah-compliant?

This solver adheres to Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions (AAOIFI) Shariah Standard No. 12 (Sharika/Musharaka) and Standard No. 9 (Ijara), verified by independent Shariah Supervisory Boards (SSBs).

Can the homeowner pay off the partnership early without penalties?

Yes. Under AAOIFI rules, the homeowner possesses the unilateral right (Wa'ad) to buy out the remaining bank equity units at face value at any time without punitive prepayment penalties.
