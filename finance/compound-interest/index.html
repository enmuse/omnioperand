Mathematical Rigor & Closed-Form Formulations

1 Discrete Compounding & Annuity Synthesis

▼

When interest compounds m times per year at nominal annual rate r, the interest earned each period is i = r / m. Over t years, the total compounding periods are N = m · t:

FVlump = P · (1 + r / m)m · t

For regular periodic deposits PMT made at the end of each period:

FVannuity = PMT · [ (1 + i)N - 1 ] / i

Summing both terms provides the complete portfolio balance at time t:

FVtotal = P(1 + r/m)mt + PMT · [ (1 + r/m)mt - 1 ] / (r/m)

2 Annual Percentage Yield (APY) Closed Form

▼

Because interest earned intra-year itself earns interest in subsequent periods, the true annual effective rate (APY) exceeds the nominal APR:

APY = (1 + r / m)m - 1

For continuous compounding, taking the limit as m ➔ ∞ gives:

lim (m ➔ ∞) (1 + r / m)m = er ⟹ APY = er - 1

3 Target Goal Inversion Formulation

▼

To compute the exact periodic deposit PMT required to reach a specific financial goal FV* from existing principal P:

PMT = [ FV* - P · (1 + r/m)mt ] · (r/m) / [ (1 + r/m)mt - 1 ]

This closed-form solution guarantees exact mathematical convergence without iterative guessing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for compound interest with regular deposits? +
The total future value combines lump-sum compounding and ordinary annuity compounding:
FV = P(1 + r/m)ᵐᵗ + PMT · [((1 + ip)ᴺ - 1) / ip]
where P is principal, r is nominal APR, m is annual compounding frequency, t is years, PMT is periodic contribution, and ip is interest rate per deposit interval.
How does compounding frequency impact returns? +
The more frequently interest is calculated and added to the principal balance (daily vs monthly vs annually), the faster interest begins earning interest on itself. For an 8% return on $10,000 over 20 years, annual compounding yields $46,610, monthly yields $49,268, and continuous compounding yields $49,530.
What is the difference between APR and APY? +
APR (Annual Percentage Rate) is the nominal stated rate without intra-year compounding. APY (Annual Percentage Yield) reflects the true effective annual yield: APY = (1 + r/m)ᵐ - 1. For 7% compounded daily, APY is 7.25%.
What is the Rule of 72? +
The Rule of 72 is an analytical shortcut to estimate how many years it takes for an investment to double at a constant annual rate: Years to Double ≈ 72 / Interest Rate. For example, at an 8% return, your money doubles in approximately 72 / 8 = 9 years.
