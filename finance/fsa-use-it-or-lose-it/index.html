Frequently Asked Questions

Can you have both an HSA and an FSA? +
You cannot have a general Healthcare FSA and an HSA simultaneously. However, you CAN have an HSA paired with a Limited Purpose FSA (LP-FSA) restricted exclusively to dental and vision expenses.
What happens if you leave your job with an unspent FSA balance? +
Any unspent FSA balance is forfeited upon termination of employment unless you elect COBRA to continue the FSA through the end of the plan year.
