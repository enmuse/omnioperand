Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ijara Muntahiya Bittamleek?

It is an Islamic lease agreement that culminates in ownership transfer through an independent gift (Hiba) or nominal token sale at the conclusion of the lease term, adhering strictly to AAOIFI Standard No. 9.

Who is responsible for major property maintenance during an Ijara contract?

Under Shariah law, the legal owner (the financier/trust) is responsible for structural repairs and catastrophic property insurance (Takaful), while the tenant covers routine operational maintenance.
