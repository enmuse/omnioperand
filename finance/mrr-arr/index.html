Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between MRR and GAAP Revenue? +
MRR is a non-GAAP normalized operational metric measuring monthly predictable recurring subscription commitments. GAAP revenue recognizes earned revenue according to ASC 606 standards over the service delivery period.
Should one-time setup fees be included in MRR? +
No! One-time implementation, onboarding, and professional services fees must be excluded from MRR because they do not recur.
