Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of taxpayers itemize deductions today? +
Prior to 2018, nearly 31% of US taxpayers itemized. Following the TCJA doubling of the standard deduction and the $10,000 SALT cap, fewer than 10% of households benefit from itemizing.
What is the charitable deduction 'bunching' strategy? +
Taxpayers who fall just short of the standard deduction often 'bunch' two or three years of charitable donations into a single year using a Donor-Advised Fund (DAF), allowing them to itemize in year 1 and take the standard deduction in years 2 and 3.
