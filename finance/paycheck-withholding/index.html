Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my bonus taxed at a higher rate than my normal paycheck? +
Bonuses are classified as supplemental wages. The IRS mandates a flat 22% federal withholding rate on bonuses up to $1M (37% over $1M), which may be higher or lower than your actual marginal bracket when filing your annual return.
What is the 2024 Social Security wage base limit? +
For 2024, the Social Security wage cap is $168,600. Earnings above this amount are exempt from the 6.2% Social Security tax.
