Frequently Asked Questions

Can you buy a house in the US with an ITIN number instead of an SSN?

Yes. Non-QM specialty lenders offer ITIN mortgage loans for Non-Permanent Resident Aliens, DACA recipients, and foreign nationals who have an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number issued by the IRS and file US tax returns.

What documentation is required to qualify for an ITIN mortgage in 2026?

Typical requirements under Fannie Mae DU and Non-QM guidelines include: 2 years of filed IRS tax returns (Form 1040) with W-2s or 1099s, valid government photo ID (consular card or passport), 12–24 months of bank statements, and 3 to 6 months of post-closing liquid reserves.

Why are ITIN mortgage interest rates slightly higher than conforming loans?

Because ITIN loans cannot be sold directly to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac as conforming agency paper, private Non-QM secondary market investors require a 0.75% to 1.75% risk premium.
