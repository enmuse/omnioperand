Analytical Proof & Invariants

Machine Precision
Governing Mathematical Formula:
EOQ = √[(2 · Demand · Order Cost) / Holding Cost per Unit]

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Ford-Harris Cost Trade-Off Balance

Annual Ordering Cost = (D / Q) · S. Annual Carrying/Holding Cost = (Q / 2) · H. Total Cost C(Q) = (D/Q)S + (Q/2)H.

2
Calculus First-Derivative Optimization

Setting dC/dQ = -DS/Q² + H/2 = 0 yields the iconic Wilson-Harris formula: EOQ = √[(2 · D · S) / H].

3
Reorder Point Formulation

Reorder Point ROP = (Daily Demand × Lead Time Days) = [(Annual Demand / 365) × Lead Time Days].

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if you order more units than the EOQ? +
Ordering above the EOQ reduces ordering frequency and administrative costs, but increases warehouse carrying costs, insurance, and the risk of obsolescence.
Does EOQ account for supplier bulk quantity discounts? +
Classic EOQ assumes constant purchase price. When suppliers offer tier discounts (e.g. 5% off at 2,000 units), you compare total costs at each price break tier against the EOQ.
