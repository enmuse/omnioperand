Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
The Net Present Value Equilibrium

Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is the discount rate r that equates the present value of all future cash flows to the initial equity: 0 = -CF_0 + ∑ [CF_t / (1 + r)^t].

2
Equity Multiple Formulation

Equity Multiple = (Total Cash Distributions + Terminal Sale Proceeds) / Initial Equity Invested. For example: ($210,000 + $820,000) / $500,000 = 2.06×.

3
Time-Value Sensitivity

A 2.0× Equity Multiple in 3 years generates ~26% IRR; that same 2.0× in 10 years yields only ~7.2% IRR. IRR penalizes capital that takes longer to return.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a good IRR for commercial real estate syndications? +
Private equity real estate sponsors typically target a 14% to 18% net LP IRR for value-add multifamily, and 18% to 22%+ for ground-up opportunistic developments over a 3 to 7-year hold period.
Why is Equity Multiple needed alongside IRR? +
IRR can be gamed through early refinances or short flips (e.g. 50% IRR on a 6-month flip might only generate a 1.25× multiple with small net dollar profit). Equity multiple measures how much total cash wealth was actually created.
