Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 4% rule? +
The 4% rule comes from the 1998 Trinity Study, proving that a portfolio invested in 50/50 to 75/25 stocks and bonds has a 95%+ probability of surviving a 30-year retirement without depletion when withdrawing 4% initially and adjusting for inflation annually.
