Frequently Asked Questions

Can you appeal an IRMAA surcharge? +
YES! If your income dropped due to a qualifying Life-Changing Event (marriage, divorce, death of spouse, retirement, or work reduction), you can file IRS Form SSA-44 to recalculate premiums based on current-year income.
How can retirees avoid the IRMAA cliff? +
Retirees utilize strategic Roth conversions prior to age 63, Qualified Charitable Distributions (QCDs) for RMDs, and drawing from non-taxable cash reserves to keep MAGI just $1 below IRMAA tier cliffs.
