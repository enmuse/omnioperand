Governing Mathematical Formula:

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Internal Rate of Return (IRR) Root Finding

IRR is the annualized discount rate r that equates the net present value of all negative and positive cash flows to zero: 0 = -C₀ + Σ [ C_t / (1 + IRR)ᵗ ].

2
2. Multiple on Invested Capital (MOIC)

MOIC measures total dollar efficiency without time discounting: MOIC = (Sum of all operating distributions + Exit proceeds) / Initial equity.

3
3. Cash-on-Cash Return

Average cash-on-cash yield measures pure operating cash flow divided by equity: CoC = (Average Annual Operating Cash Flow / Initial Equity) × 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good IRR for commercial real estate syndications? +
Private equity real estate syndications typically target a 14% to 18% levered net IRR to limited partners (LPs) over a 3 to 7-year holding period, with an equity multiple of 1.7x to 2.2x.
What is the difference between IRR and Equity Multiple (MOIC)? +
Equity Multiple tells you how much money you made in total (e.g., 2.0x doubles your money), while IRR tells you how quickly you made it. An investment doubling your money in 3 years yields ~26% IRR, while doubling in 10 years yields ~7.2% IRR.
How does the exit cap rate affect equity IRR? +
The exit sale price represents 60% to 80% of total investor returns. An increase in exit cap rate (cap rate expansion) lowers the terminal valuation and sharply decreases the final IRR.
