Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between mezzanine debt and second mortgage debt? +
A second mortgage holds a junior recorded lien against the physical real estate. Mezzanine debt does NOT hold a mortgage on the property; it holds a pledge of equity shares in the entity that owns the property, enabling faster foreclosure under the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC).
When do real estate developers use mezzanine financing? +
When senior lenders limit loans to 60-65% LTV, but the developer does not want to raise dilutive outside equity. Mezzanine debt bridges the gap up to 80-85% LTV.
