Frequently Asked Questions

Who is exempt from the 10-year inherited IRA rule? +
Eligible Designated Beneficiaries (EDBs) are exempt: 1) Surviving spouses, 2) Minor children of the deceased (until age 21), 3) Disabled individuals, 4) Chronically ill individuals, and 5) Beneficiaries not more than 10 years younger than the deceased.
What is the rule for inherited Roth IRAs? +
Inherited Roth IRAs are also subject to the 10-year rule, BUT withdrawals are 100% tax-free! For Roths, you should leave 100% of the money in the account to compound tax-free for the full 10 years before taking a single lump sum.
