Frequently Asked Questions

What is an average freemium conversion rate for SaaS? +
Standard freemium conversion rates range between 2% and 5%. Companies with strict usage gating (e.g. Dropbox, Slack) achieve 4% to 8%, while lightweight freemium apps often see 1% to 2%.
How can a SaaS improve free-to-paid conversions? +
Introduce clear usage paywalls (seat limits, feature locks, export watermarks), time-limited pro trials, and in-app onboarding triggers when users hit high-intent milestones.
