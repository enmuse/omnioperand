Frequently Asked Questions

What does paying taxes 'in arrears' mean? +
Taxes paid in arrears are billed at the end of the tax period for time already passed (common in most US states). At closing, the seller gives the buyer a cash credit for the portion of the year the seller occupied the home.
What is the difference between a 360-day and 365-day proration? +
A statutory 360-day proration treats every month as exactly 30 days (common in commercial lending). A 365-day calendar proration counts exact days.
