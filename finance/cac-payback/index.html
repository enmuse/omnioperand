Governing Mathematical Formula:
Payback Period (Months) = CAC / (Monthly ARPA × Gross Margin %)

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Monthly Margin Contribution

Net Margin Contribution per Account = ARPA × (Gross Margin% / 100). For example: $250 × 80% = $200 gross margin per month.

2
Direct Payback Horizon Equation

Payback Months = CAC / Net Monthly Margin. With $2,400 CAC and $200 monthly margin: 2,400 / 200 = 12.0 months.

3
Capital Velocity Implication

A 12-month payback allows a company to recycle the same dollar of acquisition capital annually to fund compounding growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is CAC payback preferred over LTV:CAC by CFOs? +
LTV relies on future churn predictions that may take 3 to 5 years to validate. CAC payback is immediate and cash-flow concrete: it tells you exactly when acquisition cash is back in your bank account.
What is an ideal payback period for SMB vs Enterprise? +
For SMB SaaS ($50-$500/mo), payback should be 6 to 12 months due to higher churn risk. For Enterprise ($25k-$100k+ ACV), 12 to 18 months is acceptable because enterprise churn is historically low.
