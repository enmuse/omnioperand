Frequently Asked Questions

Why is employee turnover so expensive? +
Direct recruiting fees are only the tip of the iceberg. The hidden costs—departing employee knowledge loss, team overtime, interview disruption, and the 6-month ramp-up time of the replacement—account for over 70% of total turnover expense.
What is considered a healthy corporate turnover rate? +
Across all industries, a healthy annual employee turnover rate is approximately 10% (composed mostly of natural attrition and managing out low performers).
