Frequently Asked Questions

What is cumulative vs non-cumulative preferred return? +
A cumulative pref means if property cash flow is insufficient to pay the full 8% in Year 1, the unpaid balance accrues into Year 2 and must be paid in full before the GP can ever receive promote distributions.
What is a GP catch-up clause? +
A catch-up clause allows the GP to receive 50% to 100% of distributions immediately following the preferred return until the GP's share matches their promote percentage on total profits.
