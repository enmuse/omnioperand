Frequently Asked Questions

What are the dimensions of the Great Pyramid of Giza? +
The Great Pyramid of Giza originally had a base side of ~230.3 meters (~755.6 ft) and a height of ~146.6 meters (~481 ft), yielding a volume of ~2.59 million cubic meters.
What is the difference between slant height and pyramid height? +
Vertical height is the perpendicular altitude from the apex straight down to the base center. Slant height is the altitude running along the sloping surface of an exterior triangular face to the edge midpoint.
