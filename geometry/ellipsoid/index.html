Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Ellipsoid Volume Integral

Subjecting the unit sphere x² + y² + z² ≤ 1 to the linear stretch matrix diag(a, b, c): V = 4/3 · π · a · b · c. This formula is exact for all ellipsoids.

2
Knud Thomsen Surface Area Formula

Because exact surface area requires incomplete elliptic integrals of the first and second kind, Knud Thomsen devised the formula: Area ≈ 4π · [((ab)ᵖ + (ac)ᵖ + (bc)ᵖ) / 3]^(1/p), where optimal parameter p = ln(3)/ln(2) ≈ 1.6075.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shape of planet Earth? +
Due to centrifugal forces from axial rotation, Earth is not a perfect sphere but an oblate spheroid (an ellipsoid with a = b = 6,378.1 km at the equator and c = 6,356.8 km at the poles).
What is the difference between a prolate and oblate ellipsoid? +
A prolate spheroid (football shape) has a = b < c (elongated along its polar axis). An oblate spheroid (flattened disk) has a = b > c.
