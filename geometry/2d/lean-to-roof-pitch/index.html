Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
1. Trigonometric Pitch & Angle

Roof pitch is expressed as inches of vertical rise per 12 inches of horizontal run: Pitch = (Rise / Run) × 12. Slope angle θ = arctan(Rise / Run) × 180 / π.

2
2. Hypotenuse Rafter Length

By the Pythagorean Theorem, rafter span hypotenuse = √(Run² + Rise²). Adding the overhang extension along the slope gives Total Rafter Length = (Run + Overhang/12) / cos(θ).

3
3. Miter Plumb and Seat Cut Invariants

The plumb cut at the top ledger board is exactly (90° - θ). The horizontal birdsmouth seat cut resting on the wall plate is exactly θ.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum recommended pitch for a lean-to roof? +
For standard asphalt shingles, the minimum slope is 2:12 to 4:12 with double underlayment. For metal standing seam or corrugated panels, lean-to roofs can be built as low as 1:12 or 2:12.
How do you calculate rafter length for a 12 ft run and 4 ft rise? +
By the Pythagorean theorem: √(12² + 4²) = √(144 + 16) = √160 ≈ 12.65 ft (12 ft 7-3/4 in). If you add a 12-inch horizontal overhang, the total rafter blank needed is approximately 13 ft 8-1/2 in.
What is the birdsmouth cut on a lean-to rafter? +
A birdsmouth cut is a triangular notch cut into the underside of the rafter so it rests flat on top of the supporting wall. The horizontal cut is the 'seat' and the vertical cut is the 'heel'.
