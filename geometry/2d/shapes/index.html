General Regular n-gon Mathematical Axioms

For any regular polygon with n sides of length a:

  • Interior Angle: \alpha = \frac{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}{n} = \pi \left(1 - \frac{2}{n}\right) \text{ rad}
  • Apothem (Inradius): r_i = \frac{a}{2 \tan(\pi / n)}
  • Circumradius: R = \frac{a}{2 \sin(\pi / n)}
  • Total Area: A = \frac{n a^2}{4 \tan(\pi / n)} = \frac{1}{2} n R^2 \sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{n}\right)
  • Total Diagonals: N_d = \frac{n(n - 3)}{2}
