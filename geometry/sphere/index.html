Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

1
Archimedes' Masterpiece: On the Sphere and Cylinder

Archimedes proved in 225 BC that a sphere inscribed inside a cylinder has exactly two-thirds the volume and two-thirds the surface area of the cylinder: V_sphere = ⅔ · V_cylinder = ⅔ · (πr² · 2r) = 4/3 · π · r³.

2
Surface Area Derivative

The derivative of volume with respect to radius equals the surface area: dV/dr = d/dr(4/3 π r³) = 4πr².

3
Isoperimetric Supremacy

Among all three-dimensional shapes enclosing a given volume, the sphere possesses the absolute minimum surface area, minimizing surface tension energy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the sphere surface area exactly four times its great circle area? +
A great circle has area πr². Archimedes showed that wrapping the sphere's surface tightly onto a flat plane equals exactly four great circles: 4 × πr² = 4πr².
What was carved on Archimedes' tombstone? +
Archimedes considered his proof of the 2:3 ratio of the volume and surface area of an inscribed sphere to its bounding cylinder his greatest achievement, requesting that a sphere inscribed in a cylinder be sculpted onto his tombstone.
